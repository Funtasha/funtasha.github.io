How to make it your own

The best birthday wish sounds like you, not like a greetings card. Use one of these as a starting point, then make it yours:

  • Mention a shared memory only the two of you would remember.
  • Name an inside joke or a family story you both love.
  • If you live far apart, say when you hope to see each other next.
  • Keep it light and honest - cousins can always tell when a message is forced.

Questions people ask

What should I write in a birthday card for my cousin?

Mention a shared memory - a childhood adventure, a family holiday or an inside joke - and say what their friendship means to you. Cousins love messages that show you remember the good times.

How can I make a birthday wish for my cousin more personal?

Name a specific memory only the two of you share, like a school trip or a family party. One real detail turns a standard wish into something only you could write.

Is a funny birthday message good for a cousin?

Yes, cousins are usually the perfect audience for a joke. Keep it kind and light - a gentle tease about a shared memory works well, but avoid anything embarrassing in front of others.

What is a good birthday wish for a cousin I do not see often?

Say you miss them, mention a favourite memory and suggest getting together soon. A warm message can bridge a long gap better than a long silence.

Should a birthday wish for a cousin be long or short?

Either works. A short wish is perfect for a text or social media, while a longer one suits a card. What matters is that it sounds like you and shows you care.
