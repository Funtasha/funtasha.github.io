Short new-baby wishes for new grandparents

Quick wishes for a text, a gift tag, or a card.

  • Congratulations on your beautiful new grandchild!
  • Welcome to grandparenthood - congratulations!
  • A new grandbaby! So happy for you both.
  • Congratulations, grandparents! Enjoy every cuddle.
  • Your family just grew by two tiny feet. Congratulations!

These wishes are starting points, not finished messages. Add the grandchild's name, or a line about what the grandparents mean to you, and the wish becomes personal.

How to make a wish your own

Pick a wish that matches the grandparents' sense of humour, then add the baby's name and a detail about them - how long they waited for this grandchild, or what kind of grandparents you know they will be. They will treasure a message that names their new role.

A short wish suits a text or a social media comment, while a longer one fits a card. If the grandparents live far from the baby, a message with a photo of the little one makes their day.

Questions people ask

What do I write to new grandparents?

Congratulate them on their new grandchild, say how wonderful they will be as grandparents, and share your joy for the whole family. A warm, short message is perfect.

Is a funny wish okay for new grandparents?

Yes, a gentle joke about spoiling the baby or being the favourite grandparent usually makes them laugh. Keep it kind and affectionate.

Should I mention the baby's name?

Yes, if you know it. Naming the grandchild makes the wish feel personal and shows you are celebrating this particular little person.

What if the grandparents live far away?

A message is even more important then. Send a warm wish and, if you can, a photo of the baby - it bridges the distance beautifully.

Can I send a wish to grandparents I do not know well?

Yes, a simple congratulatory wish is always appropriate. Keep it warm and short, and let the congratulations speak for themselves.
