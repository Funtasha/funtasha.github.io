What Day of the Week Were You Born?

Enter any date from 1 January 1900 to 31 December 2100 and this tool tells you the weekday it fell on. It also works out how many days old that date makes you today, and the weekday of your next birthday.

This tool uses the Gregorian calendar only, and it covers dates from 1 January 1900 to 31 December 2100. Dates before 1900 are out of scope, and leap-day birthdays in non-leap years are placed on 28 February, as explained below.

Why the weekday of a date never changes

Every date on the calendar sits on one fixed weekday, and it stays there forever. The calendar is a repeating pattern of seven-day weeks laid over the months and years, so once a date is set, its weekday is set with it. That is why a tool like this one can answer the question at all: it simply looks the date up in the calendar your browser already knows, instead of guessing.

The weekday is worked out from the date itself, not from anything about the person. Two people born on the same day share the same weekday, whether they were born in the same town or on opposite sides of the world. The only thing that matters is the date, and the calendar does the rest.

Fun ways to use your weekday

Your birth weekday makes a lovely detail for a birthday party. You could hold the celebration on the same weekday you were born on, or put the weekday on the invitation as a piece of trivia for guests to guess. A "born on a Friday" note on a birthday card is a small touch that usually gets a smile, because most people have never been told which weekday they arrived on.

It also works well as a party game. Ask each guest to guess their own birth weekday before anyone checks, then run the tool and see who was closest. Families enjoy comparing weekdays too: a parent and child who share a birth weekday, or a couple whose birthdays always land on the same weekday in a given year, have a ready-made talking point for the dinner table.

The days-old number is fun in its own right. Round numbers make good milestones: reaching 10,000 days old is a cheerful excuse for a cake, and the days-old count shows you when a round number is coming up.

Questions people ask

How far back can I check a birth date?

This tool works for dates from 1 January 1900 to 31 December 2100. Dates outside that range are not accepted, because the tool is built for birthdays people actually celebrate.

How is my age in days counted?

The tool counts whole calendar days from the date you enter up to today. A date one week ago gives 7 days, and a date entered for today gives 0 days.

What happens if I was born on 29 February?

In years without a leap day, this tool places your next birthday on 28 February and says so in the result. Many leap-day babies celebrate on 28 February, and some prefer 1 March.

Does the weekday ever change for a date?

No. Once a date is fixed on the calendar, its weekday never changes. The 1st of January 2000 was a Saturday, and it will always have been a Saturday.

Can I check a date in the future?

Yes, as long as it falls inside the range up to 31 December 2100. For a future date the tool shows its weekday and tells you how many days away it is, instead of an age.
