Zodiac Compatibility Checker: Do Your Signs Match?

Pick two star signs and this checker gives you a fun compatibility score from the four elements — fire, earth, air, and water. The score and verdict come from the element pairing alone, and the result is for fun and reflection only.

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. Sun signs do not predict personality or relationships, and this result is for fun and reflection only — never a real judgement about two people.

How the score works

Each of the twelve signs belongs to one of four elements: fire, earth, air, or water. This checker looks only at the two elements. Two signs of the same element score 85 to 95, which the tradition calls a great match. Fire with air and earth with water score 80 to 95, also a great match. The other mixed pairs — fire with earth, fire with water, air with earth, and air with water — score 55 to 70, which the tradition calls a challenging match with room to grow.

The exact number inside the band is worked out from the two sign names, so the same pair always gives the same score. Check Aries and Leo today and again next week and you will get the same number both times. Nothing is random, and nothing is read from your birthday or your birth chart — only the two signs you pick.

The sign dates on this page follow the tropical zodiac, the system most Western astrologers use. Note that the dates can shift by a day from year to year, so a birthday right on a boundary can belong to either neighbouring sign. If that is you, try both and enjoy whichever verdict makes you smile.

Fun ways to use your result

This checker works best as a party game or a conversation starter. Compare signs around the dinner table, check a couple before their anniversary, or see what the tradition says about you and your best friend. The verdict paragraphs are written to be read aloud — they describe the pairing in general terms, so everyone at the table can laugh about the parts that fit and argue cheerfully about the parts that do not.

You can also use the result as a small note in a birthday card or a gift tag. A high score makes a cheerful line, and a low score makes a better joke, because the tradition calls it a challenging match with room to grow. Either way, the number is a prompt for a smile, not a verdict on a real relationship.

Questions people ask

How is the compatibility score worked out?

The score comes from the element pairing only. Two signs of the same element score 85 to 95. Fire with air and earth with water score 80 to 95. The other mixed pairs — fire with earth, fire with water, air with earth, and air with water — score 55 to 70. The exact number inside the band is fixed by the two sign names, so the same pair always gets the same score.

What if my birthday is on the edge of two signs?

Sign dates can shift by a day from year to year, so a birthday on a boundary date can fall in either sign. Try both signs and see which verdict you enjoy more — this is a game, not a test, and there is no wrong answer.

Does a low score mean two people do not get along?

No. The score comes from an old tradition's element pairings, and it says nothing about real people. Real relationships are built on kindness, time, and honesty, not on star signs.

Why do some pairs score higher than others?

The tradition pairs elements it says work well together: fire with air, earth with water, and each element with itself. Mixed pairs get a lower band and are described as a challenging match with room to grow. The bands are part of the tradition, not measurements of anything real.

Is this the same as a horoscope?

No. This checker only compares two sun signs by their elements. It does not read birth charts, and it makes no predictions of any kind.
