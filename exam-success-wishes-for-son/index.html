These wishes are starting points — your own encouragement, in your own words, will mean the most to your son, so add a detail only you know.

How to personalize your message

The most encouraging message is a personal one. Add a detail only you know — how hard your son studied, a subject he mastered, or a moment you saw him keep going — and remind him you will be thinking of him on the day. Keep it short, warm, and calm, and avoid piling on pressure: your son needs confidence, not more worry. A message sent the night before is perfect.

Questions people ask

What should I write to my son before an exam?

Tell him you are proud of his effort, remind him of how hard he has worked, and wish him a calm mind on the day — encouragement matters most.

How do I calm my son's exam nerves?

Remind him that one exam does not define him, that you believe in him, and that you will be proud of him whatever the result.

Should exam wishes for a son be funny or serious?

A mix works well — a warm encouraging line plus a gentle joke can ease nerves, as long as the joke stays kind.

When should I send a good-luck exam message to my son?

The evening before or the morning of the exam is ideal, when nerves are highest and encouragement helps most.

What should I say to my son after the exam?

Ask how it went, listen without judging, and remind him you are proud of his effort whatever the result.
