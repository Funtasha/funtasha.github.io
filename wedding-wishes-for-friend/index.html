How to personalize your wish

The best wedding wish for a friend sounds like you on your best day — warm, a little playful, and completely sincere. Pick a wish, then add one memory only the two of you share.

  • Mention a moment from your friendship, like the day they told you about their partner.
  • Name something you love about the couple together — their laughter, their kindness, their teamwork.
  • If you are writing in a card, leave room for a short handwritten line at the end.

Questions people ask

What is a good wedding wish for a friend?

A good wish is warm, personal, and honest. Mention something you love about the couple, and wish them a marriage full of the things that matter most to them.

Should a wedding wish be funny or heartfelt?

Either can be beautiful, as long as it fits your friendship. A funny line works when you two always laugh together; a heartfelt one suits a card they will keep for years.

How do I copy a wish from this page?

Tap the Copy button under any wish to save it to your clipboard, then paste it into a card, a message, or a wedding guest book note.

Can I change the words before sending?

Yes, and you should. Add their names, a shared memory, or a detail about the day, and the wish will feel like it came from you.

Is it better to send a wish or write it in a card?

A handwritten card is the loveliest option for a wedding, but a thoughtful message on the day is wonderful too. If you can, do both — a message on the day and a card to keep.
