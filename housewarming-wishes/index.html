How to personalize your message

Add one detail about their new home to make your wish personal — a room you have seen, a garden you admire, or a feature you love. If you helped them move, mention it with a smile, and if you have not seen the house yet, say you cannot wait to visit. A warm wish plus a real detail is all it takes, and a short note on a gift tag works just as well as a long card.

Questions people ask

What should I write in a housewarming message?

Congratulate them on the new home, wish them happiness there, and mention something you like about the house or the move.

How do I make a housewarming message personal?

Mention a room you have seen, a feature you admire, or a memory of helping them move — one real detail makes it feel genuine.

Should housewarming wishes be funny or serious?

Either works — warm wishes suit a formal card, while a light joke about boxes or neighbours suits friends and family.

When should I send a housewarming wish?

Soon after they move in is best, ideally within the first few weeks, while the new home still feels exciting.

Can I send a housewarming message with a gift?

Yes — a short message on a gift tag or card makes any housewarming gift feel more thoughtful and personal.
