These wishes are starting points, not finished messages. Add the baby's name, or a line about the parents, and the wish becomes personal.

How to make a wish your own

Choose a wish that suits the parents, then add the baby's name and one detail about the family - a hope for the little boy, or a memory of the parents before he arrived. A named, specific wish is one the family will keep.

A short wish suits a text or a gift tag, while a longer one fits a card. If you know the parents well, mention something you look forward to - meeting the baby, or watching him grow.

Questions people ask

What do I write for a new baby boy?

Congratulate the parents, welcome the baby boy by name if you know it, and add a warm hope for his future. A sincere, short message is always welcome.

Should I mention that it is a boy?

Yes, mentioning the baby boy makes the wish specific to this arrival. The wishes on this page are written for a boy, so they already do that for you.

Is a funny wish okay for a new baby boy?

Yes, a light joke about boys, mud, or toy cars usually makes new parents smile. Keep it gentle and keep the congratulations at the centre.

What if I do not know the baby's name yet?

Use a general wish and add the name later. A warm message sent in the first days means more than a perfect message sent late.

Can I use these wishes on a gift tag?

Yes, the short wishes fit gift tags well. Add the baby's name and your own name to finish it.
