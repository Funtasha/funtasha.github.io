Anniversary Wishes for Parents

Your parents' anniversary deserves words as warm as the love they have shown you. This page has 25 original anniversary wishes for parents - sweet, funny and short - each with a copy button so you can send the one that fits them best.

Short anniversary wishes for your parents

Quick wishes for a text, a gift tag, or a card.

  • Thank you for showing me what true love looks like.

These wishes are starting points, not finished messages. Add a memory of your parents together, or a thank-you for something specific they did, and the wish becomes truly yours.

How to make a wish your own

Pick a wish that matches your parents' sense of humour, then add a detail from your own family life - the holiday you all still talk about, their favourite meal together, or the way they still hold hands. Parents treasure messages that show you noticed their love.

If you are sending the wish in a family group chat, keep it short so it reads well on a phone. For a card, add your name and the names of any siblings or grandchildren who want to join in.

Questions people ask

What should I write in my parents' anniversary card?

Thank them for the love they have shown each other and your family, mention a happy memory, and wish them many more years together. A few honest lines are worth more than a long formal message.

Is a funny anniversary wish okay for parents?

Yes, if your parents enjoy a laugh. A gentle joke about their habits or your childhood is often the message they remember and retell.

What if my parents are celebrating a big milestone?

Name the number of years in your message and say what their marriage has meant to you. Milestone anniversaries are a good time to say the big things you usually leave unsaid.

Can I send an anniversary wish to my parents by text?

Yes, the short wishes work well as texts. Add a family photo if you have one handy - it makes the message feel even warmer.

Should I mention both parents in the wish?

Yes, an anniversary wish is for the two of them together. Address it to both, and mention something you love about them as a couple.
