How to personalize your message

The best promotion message names something real. Mention a project your colleague handled well, a moment they helped the team, or a quality you admire in their work, and wish them well in the new role. Keep it warm and professional, and add a light joke only if you know them well. A message sent on the day of the announcement feels most genuine.

Questions people ask

What should I write to a colleague who got promoted?

Congratulate them warmly, mention something specific they did well, and wish them success in the new role — a personal detail makes it memorable.

How formal should a promotion message to a colleague be?

Match your working relationship — warm and respectful is right for most colleagues, with a light joke only if you know them well.

Should I mention their hard work in a promotion wish?

Yes — recognising the effort behind the promotion is the kindest part of the message, and it shows you noticed.

When should I send a promotion congratulations message?

As soon as you hear the news is best, while the excitement is fresh and your message feels genuine.

What if I also wanted the promotion?

Be gracious and congratulate them anyway — a kind word costs nothing and says a lot about you.
