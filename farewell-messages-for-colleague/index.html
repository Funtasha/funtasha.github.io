Farewell Messages for a Colleague

Here are 28 original farewell messages for a colleague, grouped warm, funny and short so you can find the right goodbye. Tap Copy on any message to send it in a card, a team note or a leaving gift.

Saying goodbye to a colleague is a mix of happiness for their next chapter and sadness at losing a familiar face at the next desk. A good farewell message thanks them for the shared work and the shared laughs, and wishes them well without making the goodbye heavier than it needs to be.

Warm wishes

  • Thank you for being such a wonderful colleague. Your patience, your good humour and your steady advice made every hard week easier, and you will be missed more than you know.
  • It has been a real pleasure working with you. You brought calm to busy days and kindness to every team effort, and I wish you every success in your next chapter.
  • Goodbye and good luck. Your ideas, your energy and your way of making everyone feel welcome will be hard to replace, and the office will feel different without you.
  • Thank you for all the help, the laughter and the honest conversations over coffee. I hope your new role brings you everything you are hoping for and more.
  • Working with you has been one of the best parts of this job. You made the difficult days lighter and the good days better, and I will miss you greatly.
  • Wishing you all the best in your new adventure. You have left a mark on this team that will last long after your desk is cleared, and I am grateful for every day we worked together.
  • Goodbye, and thank you for being the colleague everyone could count on. Your kindness, your hard work and your sense of humour made this place better every single day.
  • It is hard to say goodbye to someone who made work feel like teamwork. I hope your next role values you as much as we do, and I hope our paths cross again soon.
  • Thank you for your support, your ideas and your friendship. You have taught me so much about working well with others, and I will carry those lessons with me.
  • Good luck in your new role. You are leaving behind a team that admires you, a desk full of memories and a coffee mug that nobody else is allowed to touch.

Funny wishes

  • Goodbye, and good luck finding a team that laughs at your jokes as much as we do. Your new colleagues have no idea what is coming, and I almost feel sorry for them.
  • You are leaving, which means the office printer has lost its only friend, the meeting room has lost its best storyteller and I have lost my favourite lunch companion.
  • Good luck in your new job. Remember us when you are famous, and remember to send cake on your birthday, because we will still be here eating biscuits.
  • Farewell, and may your new office have better coffee, shorter meetings and a chair that does not squeak. You deserve all three, and we will miss you terribly.
  • You are escaping, and I am only slightly jealous. Take the good pens, leave the bad memories and do not forget to visit us with stories from the other side.
  • Goodbye to the only person who understood my spreadsheet jokes. Your new team will learn, eventually, but it will not be the same without you here.
  • Thank you for surviving our meetings, our deadlines and our terrible office parties. Your new colleagues are lucky, and we are already planning your welcome-back lunch.
  • Farewell, and good luck. The team has voted: your leaving gift is a plant, because unlike us, it will actually follow you to your new desk.
  • You are leaving, which means I now have to make my own coffee and find my own answers. I forgive you, but only because your new job sounds wonderful.

Short wishes

  • Goodbye and good luck. It was a pleasure working with you.
  • Thank you for everything. Wishing you every success ahead.
  • Farewell, and best of luck in your new role. You will be missed.
  • Good luck on your next adventure. Keep in touch.
  • Thank you for being a wonderful colleague. All the best.
  • Goodbye for now. Wishing you happiness and success ahead.
  • It was great working with you. Best wishes for the future.
  • Farewell, and thank you for the laughs and the teamwork.
  • Good luck, and do not be a stranger. We will miss you.
These messages are written for a happy, voluntary farewell. If your colleague is leaving because of a difficult situation, keep your message warm, simple and free of jokes about escaping or being lucky to leave.

How to make it personal

A farewell message feels genuine when it carries one small detail from your time working together. A few small touches make any message on this page sound like it came from you.

  • Mention one shared memory, like a project you finished together, a funny meeting or a team lunch, so the message feels personal.
  • Name one quality you admired, such as their patience, their ideas or their sense of humour, so the praise feels real.
  • Match the tone to your relationship: warm and personal for a close colleague, polite and brief for someone you knew less well.
  • If you want to stay in touch, say so plainly and suggest a coffee or a call, rather than a vague keep in touch.
  • Keep it short if you are writing in a shared leaving card, and save the longer stories for a message of your own.

Questions people ask

What should I write in a farewell message to a colleague?

Thank them for working with you, mention one quality or memory you valued, and wish them well in their next role. A few honest sentences are more meaningful than a long speech.

Is it okay to be funny in a farewell message?

Yes, if humour fits your relationship and the departure is a happy one. Gentle jokes about the office usually land well. Keep it kind, and skip jokes if the leaving is difficult.

How long should a farewell message be?

Two or three sentences is plenty for a shared card, and a short paragraph suits a personal message. The best farewells are warm, specific and easy to read.

Should I mention staying in touch?

Yes, if you mean it. A simple suggestion, like a coffee next month, is far better than a vague promise. If you are not sure you will, keep the goodbye warm and leave it there.

What if I did not know the colleague very well?

A short, polite message is perfect. Thank them for their work, wish them well in their new role and keep it simple. Kindness does not need a long history behind it.
