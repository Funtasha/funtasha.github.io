How to personalize your wish

Your brother will remember who took the time to write something real. Pick a wish that fits your bond, then add a line that only a sibling could write.

  • Mention a memory from growing up together, like a shared adventure or a family joke.
  • Welcome his partner by name and say what you admire about the two of them together.
  • Keep the humour kind — tease gently, then land on something sincere.

Questions people ask

What is a good wedding wish for a brother?

A good wish mixes pride with humour. Mention a memory from growing up together, welcome his partner, and tell him how happy you are for them both.

Should I tease my brother in the wish?

A gentle tease is fine, as long as it is kind. One light joke about childhood, followed by something sincere, is a combination he will appreciate.

How do I copy a wish from this page?

Tap the Copy button under any wish to save it to your clipboard, then paste it into a card, a message, or a wedding guest book note.

Should I mention his partner in the wish?

Yes, a short welcome means a great deal. Name them if you can, and say what you admire about the two of them together — it makes the wish feel like it belongs to the whole family.

What makes a brother's wedding wish special?

Honesty and history. You have known him longer than almost anyone, so a wish that mentions something only a sibling would know will stand out from every other card on the table.
