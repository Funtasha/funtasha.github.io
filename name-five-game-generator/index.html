How to play Name Five

Split into teams or play one player at a time. Draw a category, start the timer, and name five things in that category before the 30 seconds run out. If the player names five, the team scores a point; if the timer beats them, the turn passes to the next player. Play to ten points, or set a target that suits your group.

Answers must fit the category, and each answer must be different — saying the same thing twice does not count. The host is the judge: if an answer is close but not quite right, the group votes. Keep it friendly and keep it moving, because the timer waits for nobody.

Tips for a great game

  • Mix easy and tricky categories so younger players get a fair turn.
  • Let children play with a longer timer if 30 seconds feels too fast.
  • Keep a notepad for disputed answers and settle them at the end of the round.
  • Play a quick practice round so everyone understands the rules.

Questions people ask

How do you play Name Five?

Draw a category, start the 30-second timer, and name five things in that category before time runs out. Score a point for each round you complete, and play to ten points or any target your group agrees on.

How many players can join in?

Any number works. Play one player at a time, or split into teams and take turns. With younger players, let a grown-up join their team and allow extra time.

What counts as a correct answer?

Any answer that fits the category and is different from the answers already given in that round. The host decides close calls, and the group can vote if anyone disagrees.

Can I play with only one group of categories?

Yes. Use the group menu to pick Everyday, Food, Animals, Places or School, and the generator will draw from that group only until every category has been used.

What happens when all the categories are used?

The generator starts a fresh round automatically, with the categories shuffled again, so no round ever plays in the same order.
