Why lite?

TikTok Lite keeps the TikTok you open every day — your For You feed, the people you follow, your messages — in an app that loads fast and uses little data. It leaves out the heavier parts of the full app and keeps a simpler set of creation tools, so the phone spends its effort on playing videos instead of running extras in the background.

The size tells the story. The latest version is 9.5 MB, while the full TikTok app is 460.7 MB — almost fifty times larger. On a phone with little storage, that gap is the whole argument: Lite fits where the full app does not, and it leaves room for everything else on your phone.

It also runs on older phones. TikTok Lite needs Android 5.0 or newer, which covers phones from 2014 onward, and it is built to keep playing on a slow or unstable connection. If your phone struggles with the full app, or your data plan is small, Lite is the version TikTok made for you.

What TikTok Lite is

TikTok Lite is the official lightweight version of TikTok, made by TikTok Pte. Ltd. for phones with little storage, little memory, or a slow connection. It is the same TikTok — the same account, the same feed, the same people you follow — in an app built to load fast and use little data. If the full TikTok app makes your phone slow, or the Play Store says your phone cannot run it, Lite is the version TikTok made for you.

It is not a copy, a clone, or a third-party remake. It comes from TikTok, it is listed on the Play Store under TikTok's name, and it signs in with your normal TikTok account. The download button on this page goes to that official listing, and the version table above shows the latest version we checked.

One thing to know: TikTok moved the app to a new listing, now called TikTok Lite - Faster TikTok. If you had an older TikTok Lite that stopped getting updates, the current listing is the one to use — the download button above goes to it, and your account and videos carry over when you sign in.

What TikTok Lite keeps — and what it drops

Lite keeps the parts of TikTok most people open the app for. You can watch your For You feed, follow creators, like and comment, share videos, and send messages. Your profile, your videos, your following list, and your saved videos are all there, because it is the same account.

What it drops is the weight. The full TikTok app carries a large set of creation and editing tools, live features, and extra sections, and each one needs memory and data. Lite keeps a simpler set of creation tools and a lighter interface, so the app opens fast and the videos start playing sooner.

If you spend your time watching videos, you will hardly notice the difference. If you make videos with the full app's newest editing tools, you will feel it — Lite is built for watching first, and making second. For an old phone, that is the right trade.

How TikTok Lite saves data

Video is the hungriest thing a phone does with data, so a lite video app has to be careful with it. TikTok Lite loads videos at a lower quality by default, which uses far less data per minute than the full app's high-quality stream. On a small phone screen the difference is smaller than you might expect, and the video starts faster too.

The app itself is also far smaller — 9.5 MB against the full app's 460.7 MB — so it takes less storage and less data to install and update. And because it does less in the background, it does not keep using data when you are not watching.

A few habits help further. Watch on Wi-Fi when you can, and save the long scrolling sessions for then. Close other apps before you watch, so nothing else is using data in the background. And if your connection is slow, give each video a second to load instead of swiping past it — a half-loaded video still costs you the data.

How to install TikTok Lite

Installing from the Play Store takes a minute, and you do not need to remove the full TikTok app first — though on a phone short on space, removing the heavy app and keeping Lite is usually the right move.

  1. Tap the Download button above. It opens the official TikTok Lite listing on the Play Store.
  2. Tap Install and wait. At 9.5 MB, the download finishes quickly even on a slow connection.
  3. Open TikTok Lite and sign in with your normal TikTok account. Your feed, following list and videos will be there.
  4. If you already have the full TikTok app and your storage is full, uninstall the full app after Lite is working.

If the Play Store says TikTok Lite is not available in your country, the listing is restricted in your region — see the note under the download area above. If it says the app is not compatible with your phone, check that your phone runs Android 5.0 or newer in Settings, under About phone.

TikTok Lite vs the full TikTok app

The two apps are the same service with different appetites. The full TikTok app is built for new phones: it loads high-quality video, runs the full set of creation tools, and takes 460.7 MB of storage before it has cached a single video. On a new phone that is fine. On an old phone it means slow opening, stuttering video, and a battery that drains fast.

Lite is built for the opposite end. At 9.5 MB it is almost fifty times smaller, it opens fast on a phone with little memory, and it keeps playing on a weak signal where the full app buffers. It runs on Android 5.0 and up, which covers phones the full app left behind years ago.

Choose Lite if your phone is more than a few years old, if storage is tight, or if your data plan is small. Choose the full app if your phone is new and you want every creation tool at full quality. There is no wrong answer — and you can switch between them any time, because both sign in to the same account.

Your account, videos and following list

Everything in TikTok lives in your account, not in the app on your phone. When you sign in to TikTok Lite with your normal TikTok account, your videos, your following list, your followers, your liked videos, and your messages are all there, exactly as you left them in the full app.

That is also why switching is safe. Installing Lite does not delete anything, uninstalling the full app does not delete anything, and moving back to the full app later does not delete anything. The only way to lose your videos is to delete them yourself, or to delete your TikTok account.

If you make videos, one note: drafts are stored on the phone, not in your account, so a draft made in the full app will not appear in Lite. Post or save any draft you care about before you switch apps.

Tips for old phones and slow data

Lite helps a lot on its own, but a few habits make an old phone feel faster still. Free up space first: delete apps you do not use, clear old photos and videos, and empty the downloads folder. A phone with no free storage runs slowly no matter which apps you install.

Inside Lite, keep video quality low and avoid long sessions on mobile data — save those for Wi-Fi. Close apps you are not using instead of leaving them running, and restart your phone once a week to clear out the background clutter that builds up over time.

Finally, keep Lite updated. TikTok releases new versions often — the version table above shows the latest we checked — and updates usually bring small speed fixes. The Play Store updates it by itself if you let it, and at 9.5 MB the update is never a burden.
