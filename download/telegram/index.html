What is Telegram?

Telegram is a messaging app for Android that is known for speed, large groups and cloud chats. You can send text messages, photos, videos, voice notes and files of almost any kind, and your chats are stored in the cloud, so they follow you from phone to tablet to computer without any backup step. Telegram is free, and it is popular with classes, communities, news channels and teams that share a lot of files.

What makes Telegram different is how much it can hold. Groups can have huge numbers of members, channels can broadcast to unlimited subscribers, and you can send files that are far larger than most other messaging apps allow. It is a good fit when your chats are busy, your files are big, or you use several devices and want everything in step.

Chats, groups and channels

A chat in Telegram is a one-to-one conversation, much like any messenger. Groups are where Telegram shines: they can hold thousands of members, with admins, pinned messages, slow mode and topics to keep big conversations organised. A group can be private, for people you invite, or public, so anyone can find it and join.

Channels are one-way broadcasts: only the channel owners post, and subscribers read. News outlets, schools, clubs and creators use channels to share updates with a large audience. To find a channel, use the search bar at the top and type what you are looking for, then tap the channel and press Join. Be careful with channels that promise money or prizes, since those are often scams — join channels you trust, and never send money to strangers.

Groups can also run polls and quizzes, which is useful for classes and clubs making a decision together. Admins can pin important messages to the top of a group so everyone sees them first, and they can set slow mode so members can only send a message every so often, which keeps a busy group readable. Replies and mentions help you follow a single conversation inside a fast-moving group.

Cloud chats and what they mean

Cloud chats are Telegram chats stored on Telegram servers, so they are available on every device where you sign in. When you send a message from your phone, it appears on your computer at the same time, and when you delete a message, it disappears everywhere. You never need to back up cloud chats yourself, because the cloud is the backup.

The other kind of chat is a secret chat, which is stored only on the two phones taking part and is protected by end-to-end encryption. Secret chats can have a self-destruct timer, and messages cannot be forwarded from them. Most everyday chats are cloud chats, which is what makes Telegram so convenient across devices, while secret chats are there when you want a private conversation that stays on one device.

Setting up Telegram step by step

Setting up Telegram is quick. Install the app from the official Play Store page, open it, and enter your phone number. Telegram sends a confirmation code by text message, and you type it in to confirm your number. Then you add your name and, if you like, a profile photo, and you are ready to chat.

  • Install Telegram from the official Play Store page and open it
  • Enter your phone number and wait for the confirmation code
  • Enter the code to confirm your number
  • Add your name and a profile photo if you like
  • Allow access to your contacts so you can find friends who use Telegram

Your phone number is your account, so keep your SIM card safe. If you lose access to your number, you can lose access to your account too. Set up two-step verification in Settings, under Privacy and Security, to add a password that protects your account even if someone gets your confirmation code.

Files and media

Telegram is generous with files. You can send photos, videos, documents, audio and almost any file type, and the size limits are far larger than most messaging apps allow. Photos can be sent as photos, which Telegram compresses, or as files, which keeps the original quality. That makes Telegram handy for sharing study notes, work documents, long videos and photo collections.

Everything you send is stored in the cloud, so you can find it again from any device. The Saved Messages chat is your own private space in Telegram: send yourself notes, links, files and reminders, and they wait for you on every device. It works as a simple notebook and file store that you always carry with you.

Bots, stickers and themes

Bots are small automated accounts inside Telegram that do a job for you: a bot can fetch the news, translate a message, set a reminder, or play a game with you. You talk to a bot like any other chat, by tapping buttons or typing commands. Stickers are large expressive pictures you send in chats, and Telegram has thousands of free sticker packs to choose from, including animated ones that move.

Themes let you change how Telegram looks, from the colours of the chat bubbles to the background pattern. You can pick a ready-made theme or make your own, and switch between light and dark modes for day and night. Small touches like these are part of why people stay with Telegram: the app feels like yours, and it works the same way on every device you own.

Privacy settings

Telegram gives you fine control over who can see and reach you. In Settings, under Privacy and Security, you can choose who sees your phone number, your last seen time and your profile photo: everybody, only your contacts, or nobody, with exceptions for people you pick. You can also choose who can add you to groups, which stops strangers pulling you into spam groups.

Blocked users cannot message you or see your details, and you can unblock them later if you change your mind. Active sessions show every device signed in to your account, and you can end a session from another device if you lose a phone. Two-step verification adds a password on top of the text code, and it is worth turning on, since it is the strongest protection for your account.

Telegram on an old phone

Telegram needs Android 6.0 or newer, and it is a fairly light app, so it runs well on many older phones. If your phone is older than that, the latest version will not install. An earlier version from the old versions section above may still work on an older Android version, but old versions can stop working if the developer ends support for them.

Telegram stores your chats in the cloud, so switching phones is easy: sign in on the new phone and everything is there. On a phone with little storage, open Settings, then Data and Storage, and set Telegram to keep less media on the device. Clearing the cache now and then also frees space without deleting your chats, since everything is safe in the cloud.

Questions people ask

Is Telegram free?

Yes. Telegram is free to download and free to use for chats, groups, channels and calls. You only pay your normal internet or mobile data charges.

How do I install Telegram?

Tap the Download button above to open the official Play Store page, then tap Install. Open Telegram when it finishes, enter your phone number, and type in the confirmation code to set it up.

Will my chats stay?

Yes. Telegram chats are stored in the cloud, so they stay when you change phones or reinstall. Sign in with the same phone number and your chats, groups and files come back.

How do I join a channel?

Use the search bar at the top of Telegram and type the name of the channel. Tap the channel in the results, then tap Join. You can leave a channel at any time from the top of the channel page.

What Android version does Telegram need?

Telegram needs Android 6.0 or newer. You can check your version in Settings, under About phone.
