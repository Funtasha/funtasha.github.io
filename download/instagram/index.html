What is Instagram?

Instagram is a social app for Android where people share photos and short videos, follow friends and creators, and message each other. It is made by Meta, the same company behind Facebook and WhatsApp. You can post a photo of your day, watch short videos called reels, share a story that disappears after a day, or send a private message to a friend.

Instagram is built around what you see: your home feed shows posts from people and pages you follow, and the explore page shows photos and videos you might like based on what you have watched before. It is used by friends, families, shops, artists, cooks and travellers, and it is one of the most popular apps in the world for sharing pictures.

Feed, reels, stories and messages

Your feed is the stream of posts from accounts you follow, shown when you open the app. You can like a post by tapping the heart, leave a comment, or share it with a friend. Reels are short vertical videos, usually with music, that you watch by scrolling — they are the fastest-moving part of Instagram, and many people open the app just to watch them.

Stories are photos or short videos that disappear after 24 hours, which makes them good for everyday moments you do not want to keep on your profile. Direct messages let you chat privately with one person or a small group, send photos and voice notes, and share posts you liked. You can also save posts you want to return to, and organise them into collections.

The Explore page is where Instagram shows you photos and videos from accounts you do not follow yet, picked to match your interests. Tap the magnifying glass icon to open it, and browse by topic to find new creators, recipes, travel ideas or styles you might enjoy. If something in Explore does not interest you, tap it and choose Not interested, and Instagram will show you less of it.

Setting up Instagram step by step

Setting up Instagram is simple. Install the app from the official Play Store page, open it, and create an account with your phone number or an email address. Pick a username, add a password, and fill in your name and a short bio so people know who you are.

  • Install Instagram from the official Play Store page and open it
  • Tap Create new account and enter your phone number or email address
  • Choose a username and a strong password
  • Add your name, a profile photo and a short bio
  • Follow a few friends to start filling your feed

Your username is how people find you, so pick one that is easy to remember and hard to confuse with someone else. If you already have an account, tap Log in instead and enter your details. Turn on two-factor authentication in Settings, under Accounts Centre, to protect your account with a code as well as your password.

Posting a photo or reel

To post a photo, tap the plus button at the bottom of the screen, pick a photo from your gallery, and choose a filter or adjust the light and colour. Write a caption, add a location if you like, and tap Share. Your photo appears on your profile and in the feeds of people who follow you.

To post a reel, tap the plus button and choose Reel, then record a short video or pick one from your gallery. You can add music, text and effects, trim the clip, and choose a cover image before you share it. Reels are shown to people beyond your followers, so a good reel can reach a wide audience. Keep it short, start with the interesting part, and make sure the sound works.

Shopping and creators

Many shops sell through Instagram, and you can browse products without leaving the app. When a post shows a product you like, tap it to see the price and details, and follow the link to the shop if you want to buy it. Shops also run their own Instagram pages with photos of new products, so following a shop you like is an easy way to see what is new.

Creators are people who post regularly about a topic they know well, such as cooking, travel, fitness or crafts. Following a few creators you enjoy is the quickest way to make your feed interesting, since their posts will appear alongside your friends. If you enjoy making things yourself, posting regularly and replying to comments is how a small account grows into a following of its own.

Privacy settings

Instagram lets you control who sees your posts and who can reach you. A private account means only people you approve as followers can see your posts and stories. You can switch your account to private in Settings, under Privacy, and you can remove followers at any time without them being told.

You can also control who can send you messages, who can mention you, and who can comment on your posts. If someone bothers you, you can block them, restrict them so their comments are hidden, or report them to Instagram. Review your tagged photos now and then, since other people can tag you in posts that appear on your profile.

Instagram on an old phone

Instagram needs Android 9.0 or newer, so phones older than that cannot run the latest version. If the full app feels slow on your phone, try Instagram Lite, the smaller official app from Meta, which keeps the feed, stories and messages in a much smaller app — you can find it in the similar apps below. An earlier version from the old versions section above may also work, but old versions can stop working if the developer ends support for them.

On a phone with little free space, Instagram can grow large because it stores photos and videos you have viewed. Free space by clearing the cache in your phone settings, under Apps, then Instagram, then Storage. Keep your app updated, since newer versions often run better on older phones than the one you have now.

Tips for saving data

Instagram uses a lot of data because photos and videos load as you scroll. The easiest saving is to turn on data saver: open Settings, go to Data usage and media quality, and switch on Data Saver, which stops videos from playing automatically and loads lower-quality media.

  • Turn on Data Saver in Settings, under Data usage and media quality
  • Watch reels on Wi-Fi when you can, and save longer browsing for home
  • Turn off autoplay for videos so nothing loads until you tap it
  • Avoid uploading large videos on mobile data — wait for Wi-Fi
  • Check how much data Instagram uses in your phone settings, and set a limit if you need one

Stories and reels are the heaviest parts of the app, so if your data is running low, stick to the photo feed and messages. Downloading nothing and uploading only on Wi-Fi will keep Instagram affordable on a small data plan.

Questions people ask

Is Instagram free?

Yes. Instagram is free to download and free to use for posting photos, watching reels and messaging. You only pay your normal internet or mobile data charges.

How do I install Instagram?

Tap the Download button above to open the official Play Store page, then tap Install. Open Instagram when it finishes and create an account or log in.

How do I post a reel?

Tap the plus button, choose Reel, and record a video or pick one from your gallery. Add music, text or effects, choose a cover image, then tap Share.

What Android version does Instagram need?

Instagram needs Android 9.0 or newer. You can check your version in Settings, under About phone.

Is there a lighter Instagram?

Yes. Instagram Lite is the official smaller app from Meta for old phones and slow data. It keeps the feed, stories and messages in a much smaller app.
