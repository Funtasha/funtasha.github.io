What is Pinterest?

Pinterest is an ideas app for Android where you discover and save photos and ideas for things you want to try: recipes, home decor, fashion, crafts, travel plans, garden projects and more. It works like a visual notebook — you see something you like, you save it, and you find it again later when you need it.

Pinterest is calmer than most social apps, because it is about interests rather than chatter. Your home feed shows ideas matched to what you like, and you can browse at your own pace without keeping up with a conversation. It is popular with cooks, makers, planners and anyone collecting ideas for a project.

Creators and small businesses use Pinterest to share their work with people who are already looking for ideas. A cook shares recipes, a maker shares craft projects, and a shop shares photos of its products. If you make things yourself, sharing your work on Pinterest can bring it to people who are planning a project just like yours.

Pins, boards and search

A pin is a photo or video with a link to its source, such as a recipe page, a shop or a tutorial. When you tap a pin, you see it bigger, with a description and a link to where it came from. A board is a collection of pins you group together, like a folder for one project or topic.

Search is how you find ideas. Type what you are looking for — a dish, a room style, an outfit — and Pinterest shows matching pins. You can also search by photo: point your camera at something you like, and Pinterest finds similar ideas. Save the pins you like to boards, and your boards become a plan you can return to.

Setting up Pinterest step by step

Setting up Pinterest is simple. Install the app from the official Play Store page, open it, and create an account with an email address or a Google account. Pick a few interests so your home feed starts with ideas you care about.

  • Install Pinterest from the official Play Store page and open it
  • Create an account with an email address or a Google account
  • Pick a few interests, such as food, home, style or travel
  • Follow a few people or topics to fill your home feed
  • Create your first board and save a pin to it

Your profile shows your boards and the pins you have saved, so other people can see what you are interested in. You can make boards secret if you want to plan something privately, like a gift or a surprise party.

Saving ideas

Saving is the heart of Pinterest. When you see a pin you like, tap Save and choose a board to keep it on, or create a new board for it. Give your boards clear names, like Weeknight Dinners or Small Balcony Ideas, so you can find them later.

You can organise a board into sections, which helps when a board grows large. A home board might have sections for the kitchen, the bedroom and the garden. You can also add notes to a pin, so you remember why you saved it, and you can invite friends to a shared board when you are planning something together.

Shopping from pins

Many pins link to products you can buy, from furniture and clothes to craft supplies and kitchen tools. When a pin shows a price, tapping it takes you to the shop that sells it, where you can see details and buy if you want to. Pinterest is a good place to compare styles before you buy, since you can save several options to a board and look at them side by side.

To shop wisely, check the shop behind a pin before you buy, especially for expensive items. Read the description, check the delivery details, and compare the price with other shops. Saving a product to a board and waiting a day before you buy is a simple habit that stops impulse buying and helps you choose the thing you really want.

  • Tap a product pin to see the price and the shop behind it
  • Save several options to a board and compare them side by side
  • Check delivery details before you buy
  • Wait a day before buying something expensive
  • Only buy from shops you trust

Following people and topics

Following is how your home feed gets better. Follow people whose taste you like, and follow topics you care about, and Pinterest shows you more of the same. You can follow a single board rather than a whole account, which is useful when someone has many boards and you only like one of them.

Your home feed learns from what you save and tap, so the more you use Pinterest, the more it matches your taste. If your feed shows things you do not like, tap the three dots on a pin and hide it, or tell Pinterest you are not interested. That feedback shapes what you see next.

The Updates tab shows you what is new from the people and topics you follow, along with messages from Pinterest about your boards. It is a quick way to catch up without scrolling through your whole feed. You can also turn notifications on or off in Settings, so Pinterest only tells you about the things you care about.

Pinterest on an old phone

Pinterest needs Android 10.0 or newer, so phones older than that cannot run the latest version. If the latest version will not install on your phone, the Play Store will offer the newest version that fits your phone instead. Pinterest is mostly photos, so it needs a decent connection to load images smoothly.

On an old phone, free up storage first, since Pinterest stores images you have viewed. Close other apps before you open it, and browse on Wi-Fi when you can. If scrolling feels slow, try the mobile website in your browser as a lighter alternative — you can browse and save pins there too.

Tips for finding ideas

The best way to find ideas on Pinterest is to search with specific words. Instead of searching for cake, try chocolate birthday cake or eggless banana cake, and the results will match what you actually want. Save generously at first, and tidy your boards later — it is easier to delete a pin than to find it again.

  • Search with specific words to get results that match what you want
  • Use visual search to find ideas that look like something you already like
  • Follow boards, not just people, to keep your feed focused
  • Make boards secret when you are planning a surprise
  • Check the link under a pin before you trust a recipe or a claim

Pins link out to other websites, so the quality of an idea depends on its source. A recipe from a well-known cook is more reliable than one from an unknown page, so tap through and check the source before you follow it.

Questions people ask

Is Pinterest free?

Yes. Pinterest is free to download and free to use for browsing, saving pins and making boards. You only pay your normal internet or mobile data charges.

How do I install Pinterest?

Tap the Download button above to open the official Play Store page, then tap Install. Open Pinterest when it finishes and create an account or log in.

How do I save a pin?

Tap a pin you like, then tap Save and choose a board to keep it on. You can create a new board from the same screen if you need one.

What Android version does Pinterest need?

Pinterest needs Android 10.0 or newer. You can check your version in Settings, under About phone.

Can I use Pinterest on an old phone?

Yes, if your phone runs Android 10.0 or newer. Free up storage first, browse on Wi-Fi when you can, and try the mobile website in your browser if the app feels slow.
