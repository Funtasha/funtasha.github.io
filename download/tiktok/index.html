What is TikTok?

TikTok is a short video app for Android where people watch and make quick videos, usually under a minute long, on every topic you can think of: cooking, comedy, music, study tips, sport, crafts and daily life. You open the app and videos start playing one after another, chosen to match what you have watched and liked before.

TikTok is made by TikTok Pte. Ltd., and it has grown into one of the most popular apps in the world. Anyone can post a video from their phone, and a video from a brand new account can reach millions of people if viewers enjoy it. That is what makes TikTok different from older social apps: you do not need followers to be seen.

Feed, following and live

The For You feed is the heart of TikTok. It shows a stream of videos picked for you, based on what you watch, like, share and comment on. The more you use it, the better it gets at showing you things you enjoy. If a video does not interest you, swipe past it, and TikTok learns from that too.

Following is the other half of the app: when you follow a creator, their new videos appear in your Following feed, so you never miss them. Live is where creators broadcast in real time and chat with viewers as they watch. You can join a live stream to ask questions, send reactions, or just watch what is happening right now.

The search page is worth exploring too. Tap the magnifying glass and type a topic, and TikTok shows videos, creators and hashtags that match. It is a quick way to learn something new, find a recipe, or see how other people do a skill you want to try. Trending searches show what a lot of people are looking for right now.

Making a short video step by step

Making a TikTok video is simple, and you need nothing more than your phone. Tap the plus button at the bottom of the screen to open the camera, then record or upload your clip.

  • Tap the plus button to open the camera
  • Record a clip, or tap Upload to pick a video from your gallery
  • Add music, text, filters or effects to your video
  • Write a caption and add hashtags so people can find it
  • Tap Post to share it with the world

Keep your first videos short and simple. Good light and clear sound matter more than fancy effects, so record near a window and speak clearly. Watch how popular videos in your topic are made, and try the same structure with your own idea. Your drafts are saved on your phone only, so finish and post a video before you switch phones.

Duets, sounds and trends

A duet is a video you make alongside someone else's video, shown side by side. People use duets to react, to sing along, to answer a question, or to add their own take to a popular clip. A stitch lets you use a short piece of another video at the start of your own, which is handy for replies and reactions. Both are easy ways to join a conversation that is already happening.

Sounds are the music and audio clips behind videos, and they are how trends spread on TikTok. When you like the sound in a video, tap it to see other videos that use it, and use it in your own video if you want to join the trend. Trends move fast, so do not worry about catching every one — pick the ones that fit what you enjoy making.

  • Try a duet to react to a video you liked
  • Use a trending sound to join a popular theme
  • Keep your videos short so people watch to the end
  • Post regularly rather than waiting for a perfect video
  • Reply to comments to keep people coming back

Privacy settings

TikTok gives you control over who sees your videos and who can reach you. A private account means only people you approve can follow you and see your videos. You can also make single videos private, or limit who can comment, send you messages, or duet with your videos.

In Settings, under Privacy, you can choose who can view your videos, who can send you direct messages, and whether strangers can find you. If someone bothers you, block them or report them from their profile. Screen time controls let you set a daily limit and take breaks, which is useful because short videos are easy to keep watching for longer than you planned.

TikTok on an old phone

TikTok needs Android 6.0 or newer, and it is a large app, so it can feel heavy on a phone with little storage or an older processor. If the full app feels slow, try TikTok Lite, the smaller official app, which keeps short videos in a much smaller package — you can find it in the similar apps below. An earlier version from the old versions section above may also work, but old versions can stop working if the developer ends support for them.

To help TikTok run better on an old phone, free up storage first: delete apps and files you do not use, and clear TikTok's cache in Settings, under Free up space. Close other apps before you open TikTok, and keep the app updated, since newer versions often fix slowness on older phones.

Tips for saving data

TikTok uses a lot of data because videos play one after another as you scroll. The built-in data saver helps: open Settings, go to Data Saver, and turn it on, which lowers video quality and stops some preloading. Watching on Wi-Fi whenever you can is the biggest saving of all.

  • Turn on Data Saver in TikTok settings to lower video quality
  • Watch videos on Wi-Fi and save your mobile data for messages
  • Avoid uploading large videos on mobile data
  • Clear the cache now and then so old videos do not pile up on your phone
  • Set a daily screen time reminder so a quick look does not become an hour of videos

Live streams use more data than recorded videos, so save them for Wi-Fi too. If your data plan is small, short sessions on Wi-Fi will keep TikTok affordable and still let you enjoy the videos you like.

If TikTok is not available in your country

TikTok is not available in every country. Some governments block it, and in some places the Play Store listing is not offered. If you open the Play Store and cannot find TikTok, that is usually the reason, and no setting on your phone will change it.

Some people use a VPN to reach apps that are blocked where they live. A VPN can make it look as if you are in another country, but it may break local rules, and free VPN apps can be unsafe, since some of them log what you do or show unwanted adverts. Check your local rules first, and if you do use a VPN, pick one you trust rather than the first free one you find.

Questions people ask

Is TikTok free?

Yes. TikTok is free to download and free to use for watching and making videos. You only pay your normal internet or mobile data charges.

How do I install TikTok?

Tap the Download button above to open the official Play Store page, then tap Install. Open TikTok when it finishes and create an account or log in.

How do I make a video?

Tap the plus button, record a clip or upload one from your gallery, add music or effects, write a caption, then tap Post to share it.

What Android version does TikTok need?

TikTok needs Android 6.0 or newer. You can check your version in Settings, under About phone.

Is there a lighter TikTok?

Yes. TikTok Lite is the official smaller app for old phones and slow data. It keeps short videos in a much smaller app that uses less storage and less data.
