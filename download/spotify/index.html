What is Spotify?

Spotify is a music and podcast app for Android that gives you a huge library of songs, albums and spoken shows in one place. You can listen to new releases, old favourites, podcasts and audiobooks, and you can do it on your phone, on a speaker, or in the car. Your account keeps your saved music and listening history together, so you can pick up where you left off on any device.

Spotify is free to start: the free plan lets you listen to music and podcasts without paying. A paid plan adds extra features, such as listening without adverts and downloading songs to play offline. Whether you listen for free or pay, the app works the same way — search for what you want, press play, and let the music run.

Spotify began as a music service and has grown to include podcasts and audiobooks, so one app now covers most of what people listen to. You can search for almost any song you can think of, follow artists to hear their new releases, and let the app suggest music based on what you already enjoy. It works on phones, tablets, computers and speakers, and your place in a podcast or audiobook is remembered wherever you listen.

Songs, albums, playlists and podcasts

Spotify organises music in a few simple ways. A song is a single track. An album is a collection of songs released together by an artist, in the order the artist chose. A playlist is a list of songs you or someone else has put together, for a mood, an activity or an occasion. You can follow playlists made by Spotify, by other listeners, or by your friends.

Podcasts are spoken shows on almost any topic: news, stories, comedy, sport, learning and more. You can follow a podcast to get new episodes as they arrive, and you can download episodes to listen without internet. If you are new to podcasts, start with a show about something you already enjoy, and let Spotify suggest others like it.

You can also save songs you like to your library, which makes them easy to find again. Saved songs appear in your collection, and you can sort them by title, artist or the date you added them. Over time your library becomes a personal record of your taste, and it is the starting point for most of Spotify's suggestions.

Making a playlist, step by step

A playlist is your own collection of songs, and making one is simple. You might make a playlist for a workout, a journey, a party or a quiet evening. The best playlists have a clear mood and a good flow, so think about what the songs have in common before you start adding them.

A good playlist has a flow. Start with a song that sets the mood, build up to the liveliest tracks in the middle, and end with something that feels like a closing. You can reorder songs at any time, and you can keep adding to a playlist for as long as you like. Some people keep one playlist for years, adding new favourites as they find them.

  • Open Spotify and tap Your Library, then tap the plus button to create a new playlist
  • Give the playlist a name that says what it is for
  • Search for a song, tap the three dots next to it, and choose Add to playlist
  • Keep adding songs until the list feels complete, then drag them into the order you want
  • Share the playlist with friends, or keep it private if you prefer

Offline listening

Offline listening means downloading songs to your phone so you can play them without internet. It is useful on a plane, on a long journey, or anywhere your signal is weak. You download over Wi-Fi at home, and the songs stay on your phone until you remove them. Downloaded songs do not use your mobile data when you play them.

Offline listening is part of the paid plan. With it, you can download whole playlists, albums or podcasts in one tap. Keep an eye on your storage: a large playlist can take a lot of room, so download what you will actually listen to and remove old downloads when you are done with them.

Recommendations and Discover Weekly

Spotify learns what you like from what you play, skip and save, and uses that to suggest new music. The more you listen, the better the suggestions get. You will see mixes made for you, new releases from artists you follow, and songs similar to ones you already love. Trying a suggestion is the easiest way to find music you would never have searched for.

Discover Weekly is a playlist Spotify makes fresh for you each week, full of new songs picked to match your taste. It is one of the best ways to find new artists. If you like a song in it, save it to your library or add it to a playlist of your own, because next week's list will be different.

Your taste profile grows as you listen. Liking songs and saving them tells Spotify what you enjoy, while skipping a song tells it what to play less. If your recommendations ever feel stale, try listening to a new genre or an artist you have never tried: a little exploration gives Spotify fresh clues, and your mixes will change with you.

Following artists and sharing music

Following an artist tells Spotify you like their work. You will see their new releases when they arrive, and your recommendations will lean towards their style. You can also follow friends and see what they are listening to, which is a friendly way to swap music tastes.

Sharing is simple: from any song, album or playlist, tap Share and send it to a friend, a group or a social app. A shared song opens straight in Spotify for the other person. If you and a friend both use Spotify, you can even build a playlist together, adding songs from both of your phones.

Data and battery tips

Streaming music uses mobile data and battery, but a few habits keep both under control. Download your favourite playlists over Wi-Fi and listen offline when you are out. Lower the audio quality in settings when you are on mobile data, and raise it again on Wi-Fi when you want the best sound.

  • Download playlists over Wi-Fi before a journey
  • Use a lower audio quality setting on mobile data
  • Close the app fully when you are done listening
  • Keep your downloads tidy and remove ones you no longer play
  • Charge your phone before a long listening session

Questions people ask

Is Spotify free?

Yes, Spotify has a free plan — you can listen to music and podcasts without paying. The free plan plays adverts and has some limits, such as shuffle-only play on some playlists. A paid plan removes the adverts and adds offline listening and on-demand play.

How do I install the Spotify APK?

Tap the Download button above to open the official Play Store page, then tap Install. If you use a file from the APKPure archive instead, open the downloaded file and allow your phone to install it when asked, then follow the on-screen steps.

How do I download songs for offline listening?

Offline listening is part of the paid plan. With it, open a playlist or album, switch on Download, and wait for the songs to save over Wi-Fi. Then you can listen without mobile data.

How do I make a playlist?

Open Spotify, tap Your Library, then tap the plus button to create a new playlist. Give it a name, then search for songs and add them with the three-dot menu next to each song.

What Android version does Spotify need?

Spotify needs Android 7.0 or newer. You can check your version in Settings, under About phone.
