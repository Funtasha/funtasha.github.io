What is Picsart?

Picsart is a photo editor for Android that covers both quick fixes and creative edits. You can crop a photo, fix its colours, add a filter, and share it in a minute — or you can spend longer building a collage, adding stickers and text, and turning an ordinary photo into something playful. It is one of the most widely used photo editors on phones, and it suits beginners and confident editors alike.

Picsart works on the photos already on your phone, and it can also open a picture straight from your camera. Your edits stay on your device until you choose to share them. The app organises its tools into clear groups, so the everyday fixes are easy to find and the creative tools are there when you want to experiment.

Picsart has grown over many years from a simple photo editor into a full creative studio, but it still keeps the basics easy. The tools you use every day — crop, filters, brightness — sit on the main editing screen, while stickers, collage and AI tools wait a tap away. That balance is why it suits both a quick fix before sharing and a longer creative project.

Crop, filters and adjust

Cropping is the simplest way to improve a photo. Cut away the empty space, the stranger at the edge, or the cluttered background, and the subject stands out. Picsart lets you crop freely or to a set shape, such as a square for a feed or a tall shape for a story. A small crop often makes a bigger difference than any filter.

Filters change the mood of a photo with one tap: warmer, cooler, brighter or more dramatic. After a filter, the adjust tools let you fine-tune the result — brightness, contrast, warmth and sharpness. Use them gently. A photo that looks natural usually beats one that looks heavily processed, and small changes are easy to undo if you go too far.

Stickers

Stickers are Picsart's most playful feature. You can add ready-made stickers — hearts, stars, labels, cartoon shapes — and place them anywhere on your photo. Resize them, rotate them, and layer them to build a fun scene. Stickers are great for birthday photos, party pictures and casual posts where a light touch is welcome.

You can also make your own stickers from any photo. Cut out a person, a pet or an object, and Picsart turns it into a sticker you can reuse on other photos. This is how people make personal sticker sets of their friends, their dog or their favourite things. Keep your own stickers simple and well cut, and they will look neat wherever you place them.

Collage and grids

A collage combines several photos into one picture. Picsart offers grids, where photos sit in neat rows and columns, and free-form layouts, where you can overlap photos and rotate them. Grids suit a set of photos from one day out, while free-form collages suit a mood board or a birthday tribute.

A good collage tells a small story. Pick photos that belong together, put the strongest one in the largest space, and keep the layout simple enough that each photo can still be seen. Avoid squeezing in too many pictures: six clear photos beat twelve tiny ones. Once the layout looks right, you can add a border, a background colour and a short caption to finish it.

Background remover

The background remover cuts the subject out of a photo and removes everything behind it. Point it at a portrait or a product photo, and Picsart separates the person or object from the background in a few seconds. You can then place the cut-out on a plain colour, a different photo, or a transparent background.

This tool is useful for profile pictures, product photos for a shop, and fun edits where you place someone in a new scene. It works best when the subject stands out clearly from the background and the edges are not too fiddly, such as loose hair against a busy scene. Check the edges after the cut and tidy any rough spots before you save.

Text on photos

Text turns a photo into a message: a birthday wish, a quote, a label or a title. Picsart offers many fonts, from plain and readable to decorative and bold, and you can change the colour, size and position of each line. You can also add a shadow or an outline to help the words stand out from a busy photo.

Keep your text short and readable. One line in a large, clear font works better than a paragraph in a fancy one. Place the words where they do not cover faces, and check the photo at a small size, because most people will see it on a phone screen. If the words are hard to read at that size, make them bigger or simpler.

AI tools in plain words

Picsart includes tools that use artificial intelligence, which means the app can do clever jobs by itself. One popular tool sharpens a blurry photo or brightens a dark one. Another can change a photo into a cartoon or painting style. A third can fill in missing parts of a picture or replace the sky behind a landscape.

These tools are fun to try, but check their results before you share them. AI can sometimes change small details, such as hands or text in a photo, in odd ways. Use the tools to improve a photo you already like, rather than to rescue one that is badly out of focus, and keep a copy of the original so you can start again if you prefer it.

Saving and sharing

When your edit is done, save it to your phone at the best quality the app offers. A high-quality save keeps your photo sharp when you print it or view it on a bigger screen, while a smaller save is fine for a quick share. Picsart lets you save to your gallery, and from there you can share the photo to any app you like.

It is worth keeping your original photo as well as the edited one. Save the edit as a new file rather than overwriting the original, so you can always go back and try a different edit later. If you plan to print a photo, check that it still looks sharp at the size you want before you send it to print.

If you share your photo on a social app, check how it looks after uploading, because some apps compress photos and soften fine detail. A photo that looks perfect in your gallery can look slightly softer online. Saving at the highest quality gives the app the best starting point, and checking the result lets you fix anything that did not survive the upload.

Tips for a clean photo edit

A clean edit looks effortless, but it comes from small, careful choices. Start with the best photo you have, fix the basics first, and add creative touches only where they help. When in doubt, do less: a photo that looks natural will still look good years from now.

  • Crop first, then fix the light, then add filters or stickers
  • Use one filter and keep its strength below full
  • Keep text short and easy to read on a phone screen
  • Check the edges after removing a background
  • Save a copy of the original before you start editing

Questions people ask

Is Picsart free?

Yes. Picsart is free to download and the main editing tools cost nothing. Some extra stickers, filters and AI tools are paid options.

How do I install the Picsart APK?

Tap the Download button above to open the official Play Store page, then tap Install.

How do I remove a photo background in Picsart?

Open your photo in Picsart and choose the background remover tool. Picsart cuts out the subject and removes the background, then you can place the cut-out on a new colour or photo.

Can I make stickers in Picsart?

Yes. Open a photo, cut out the person or object you want, and Picsart turns it into a sticker you can reuse on other photos.

What Android version does Picsart need?

Picsart needs Android 6.0 or newer. You can check your version in Settings, under About phone.
