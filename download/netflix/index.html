What is Netflix?

Netflix is a streaming app for Android where you watch movies and shows over the internet, whenever you like. There are no fixed schedules: you pick what to watch and press play. Netflix makes its own movies and shows too, alongside films and series from studios around the world, in many languages.

You watch with a paid subscription, which you set up on the Netflix website or in the app. Once you are signed in, the app works on your phone, tablet, television and computer, and you can stop watching on one device and continue on another. Your place is remembered, so you never lose your spot in a series.

Movies, shows and offline downloads

The Netflix library covers movies, series, documentaries, stand-up comedy, kids shows and more. The home screen suggests things based on what you have watched, and you can search by title, actor or genre. You can add titles to My List to watch later, and rate what you watch to improve the suggestions.

Offline downloads let you save movies and episodes to your phone and watch them without internet. That is useful on a plane, on a long journey, or anywhere your signal is weak. Downloads are part of your paid subscription, and they expire after a while, so download what you plan to watch soon and remove old downloads when you are done.

If you are not sure what to watch, the Top 10 row shows the most popular movies and shows in your country right now. It is a good starting point when everyone is asking what to watch tonight. You can also browse by genre, such as comedy, drama, documentary or action, to narrow down the choices.

Setting up Netflix step by step

Setting up Netflix is simple. Install the app from the official Play Store page, open it, and sign in with the account you use for Netflix. If you do not have an account yet, you can create one and choose a subscription plan.

  • Install Netflix from the official Play Store page and open it
  • Sign in with your Netflix account, or create a new account
  • Pick a profile, or create one for yourself
  • Browse the home screen or search for something to watch
  • Tap a title and press Play

If you share Netflix with family, each person can have their own profile, so everyone gets their own suggestions and watch history. Kids profiles show only family-friendly titles, which is useful when children use the app.

Profiles and what they mean

A profile is a personal space inside one Netflix account. Each profile has its own My List, its own watch history and its own suggestions, so what you watch does not mix with what someone else in the house watches. Most accounts can hold several profiles, including kids profiles.

To make a profile, open the profile menu and tap Add Profile, then pick a name and an icon. To keep a profile private, you can set a profile lock with a PIN, so nobody else can open it. If suggestions feel wrong, it is often because someone else has been watching on your profile — check your viewing activity in account settings.

The Continue Watching row on your home screen shows everything you started but have not finished, with your place remembered. If a title is stuck there that you no longer want, you can remove it from the row, which also tidies up your suggestions. Your viewing history in account settings lists everything you have watched on that profile.

Kids profiles and family viewing

A kids profile is a safe space for children inside a Netflix account. It shows only family-friendly titles, with a simpler look and bigger icons, and it does not show anything meant for adults. You can make a kids profile from the profile menu by choosing the kids option when you add a profile.

Parents can set controls on a kids profile, such as a maturity rating limit and a PIN that stops children switching to an adult profile. You can also see what your children have watched, which helps you talk about it with them. For family movie nights, pick a film everyone can enjoy, and use the kids profile for the youngest viewers so nothing unsuitable appears.

  • Make a kids profile for children so they only see family-friendly titles
  • Set a maturity rating limit on the kids profile
  • Use a profile lock PIN so children cannot open an adult profile
  • Check viewing activity to see what your children have watched
  • Pick family movies together so everyone enjoys the evening

Downloading for offline watching

To download a movie or episode, open it and tap the Download button. The download starts over Wi-Fi by default, and you can watch it from the Downloads section of the app. Most titles can be downloaded, though a few are not available for download because of licensing rules.

Downloads take up storage, so check how much space you have before you download a whole season. In App Settings, you can choose the download video quality: Standard uses less space, while Higher uses more space but looks sharper. Delete downloads you have finished, since they expire anyway, and download fresh copies when you need them again.

Data-saving settings

Video uses a lot of data, so Netflix gives you controls to keep it in check. In App Settings, under Cellular Data Usage, you can choose how much data Netflix uses on mobile data: Automatic, Wi-Fi Only, Save Data, or Maximum Data. Save Data is the best choice on a small plan, and Wi-Fi Only stops Netflix from using mobile data at all.

  • Set Cellular Data Usage to Save Data or Wi-Fi Only on a small plan
  • Download shows over Wi-Fi at home, then watch them offline
  • Choose Standard download quality to save storage space
  • Avoid autoplay previews, which play video as you browse
  • Check your phone settings to see how much data Netflix has used

Watching in high quality on a phone screen uses much more data for a difference that is hard to see on a small display. Standard quality looks fine on a phone and uses far less data, so it is the sensible choice when you are away from Wi-Fi.

Netflix on an old phone

Netflix needs Android 9.0 or newer, so phones older than that cannot run the latest version. If the latest version will not install on your phone, the Play Store will offer the newest version that fits your phone instead. Older phones can still stream, but the picture may be less sharp and the app may take longer to start.

On an old phone, keep your expectations modest and your storage free. Close other apps before you open Netflix, and restart the phone now and then. If videos stutter, lower the data usage setting, which also lowers the picture quality and makes playback smoother on a slow connection.

Questions people ask

Is Netflix free?

No. Netflix is a paid subscription service. The app is free to download, but you need a paid subscription to watch movies and shows.

How do I install Netflix?

Tap the Download button above to open the official Play Store page, then tap Install. Open Netflix when it finishes and sign in with your account.

How do I download shows offline?

Open a movie or episode and tap the Download button. The download saves to your phone over Wi-Fi, and you can watch it from the Downloads section without internet.

What Android version does Netflix need?

Netflix needs Android 9.0 or newer. You can check your version in Settings, under About phone.

Can I watch Netflix on an old phone?

Yes, if your phone runs Android 9.0 or newer. On an older phone, close other apps first, keep storage free, and lower the data usage setting if videos stutter.
