Why lite?

Files by Google is not a cut-down version of another app — it is Google's own light file manager, and it is the light choice for an old phone. At 19.1 MB it is small, it runs fast, and it replaces the heavier cleaner and file-manager apps that fill phones with ads and background work.

What is Files by Google?

Files by Google is Google's official file manager and storage cleaner for Android. It does two jobs in one small app: it shows you every file on your phone — photos, videos, downloads, documents — and it finds the files you do not need any more, so you can free space with a few taps. On an old phone that is always full, it is one of the most useful apps Google makes.

It is a real Google app, not a copy. It is published by Google, it is free, and it works without an account — open it and it shows your files straight away. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to set up. The app reads only the files on your own phone, and it never deletes anything without asking you first.

Files by Google is at its best on a phone with little storage. A phone that is nearly full runs slowly: apps take longer to open, the camera refuses to take photos, and updates fail for lack of room. Files finds the space for you — old downloads, duplicate photos, forgotten videos — and gives the phone room to breathe again.

Cleaning junk and freeing space

The cleaning tab is where Files earns its place. Open it and the app shows you suggestions: temporary files left behind by other apps, old downloads you forgot about, photos you already backed up, and files from chats that pile up quietly. Each suggestion shows exactly how much space it frees, so you decide what goes and what stays.

Cleaning is always your choice. Files never deletes anything on its own — it shows you the files, you pick the ones to remove, and you confirm. If a suggestion includes something you want to keep, leave it out. The app is a helper, not a boss: it finds the candidates, you make the call.

A little regular cleaning goes a long way. Open Files once a week, check the suggestions, and clear what you do not need. Five minutes a week keeps a phone from ever hitting the storage warning, and a phone with free space runs every app better — not just Files.

Finding big files and duplicates

Big files hide. A video you recorded a year ago, a downloaded film, a game you stopped playing — each one can take hundreds of megabytes, and together they fill a phone. Files sorts your files by size, so the biggest ones are easy to find. Look at the top of the list before you delete anything small: one big video is worth a hundred tiny files.

Duplicates hide too. The same photo saved twice, a file downloaded again because you forgot the first copy, a video shared to you in two different chats — Files finds files that are identical and shows them side by side, so you can keep one copy and remove the rest.

The same idea applies to blurry photos and old screenshots. Files can spot photos that are out of focus or near-identical bursts, and screenshots you took months ago and never looked at again. Clearing those is painless — you will not miss a blurry photo or a screenshot of a bus timetable from last year.

Sharing files without using data

Files by Google can send files to a nearby phone without using mobile data and without the internet. Both phones open Files, one sends and the other receives, and the file travels directly between them over a fast local connection. It works for photos, videos, apps and documents — anything on the phone.

This is handy in all sorts of everyday moments. Send a video to a friend sitting next to you, move your photos to a new phone, or share a file with someone whose data has run out. The transfer is fast, it costs no data, and it works wherever two phones are close together.

Sharing is safe by design. The receiving phone sees who is sending and what the file is before accepting, and nothing moves until both sides agree. If you have ever paid to send a big video over mobile data, this feature alone is worth installing the app for.

How to install Files by Google step by step

Installing Files by Google takes about a minute, and you do it from the official Play Store listing. Here is the whole process:

  1. Tap the Download button on this page. It opens the official Files by Google listing in the Play Store.

Files by Google vs other cleaner apps

There are many cleaner apps, and most of them are heavier than the problem they solve. They show ads, run in the background, ask for permissions they do not need, and promise speed gains they cannot deliver. Files by Google is the opposite: it is made by Google, it is free, and it does exactly two jobs — files and cleaning — and stops there.

The other difference is trust. A cleaner app can delete your files, so it matters who made it. Files by Google comes from the company that makes Android itself, it shows you every file before it deletes anything, and it never deletes without your say-so. A cleaner from an unknown maker, asking for broad access to your phone, is not worth the risk.

If you already have a cleaner app you do not trust, remove it and use Files instead. One small, honest cleaner beats a heavy one that fills your phone with pop-ups — and Files is the cleaner that comes from the source.

Tips for keeping an old phone fast

Files by Google helps most, but a few habits keep an old phone fast alongside it:

  • Open Files once a week and clear the cleaning suggestions. Five minutes a week keeps the phone from ever filling up.
  • Remove apps you never open. Every app takes storage and some run in the background.
  • Move photos and videos you want to keep to cloud backup or a computer, then remove the copies from the phone.
  • Restart your phone once a week. It clears temporary files and gives every app a fresh start.
  • Keep Files updated. New versions fix bugs and usually run better than the one before.

An old phone does not need the newest apps — it needs the lightest ones, and a little regular care. Files by Google gives you both.
