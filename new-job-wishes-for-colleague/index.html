How to personalize your wish

A good colleague wish is warm but professional — friendly enough to feel personal, respectful enough to share in a group card. Pick a wish, then add one detail from your time working together.

  • Mention something specific you worked on together, or a quality you admired in them.
  • Keep the tone upbeat and forward-looking — this is a celebration, not a goodbye.
  • If you are signing a group card, keep your line short so there is room for everyone.

Questions people ask

What is a good new-job wish for a colleague?

A good wish is warm but professional. Mention something you admired about working with them, and wish them well in the new role without making it sound like a goodbye.

Should I keep it formal or friendly?

Friendly is fine, as long as it stays respectful. A short, upbeat line works in a card, an email, or a team chat, and a touch of humour is welcome if you know them well.

How do I copy a wish from this page?

Tap the Copy button under any wish and it is saved to your clipboard, ready to paste into a message, a card, or a social post.

Can I use one of these wishes in a group card?

Yes. Pick a short one so there is room for everyone else, and add your name and one personal line so your colleague knows who wrote it.

When should I send the wish?

On their last day with you, or on the morning they start the new job. A short message on their first day is a kind way to say you are thinking of them.
