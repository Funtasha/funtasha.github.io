What Generation Am I?

Enter your birth year and this tool tells you which generation label it falls under, using one common convention from the Silent Generation to Generation Alpha. The full table is shown on the page, and years outside it get an honest message.

The generation table

This tool uses one common convention for generation labels. Other sources use slightly different years, so labels can vary — treat the result as a handy label, not a fixed identity.

YearsGeneration
1928 to 1945Silent Generation
1946 to 1964Baby Boomer
1965 to 1980Generation X
1981 to 1996Millennial
1997 to 2012Generation Z
2013 to 2024Generation Alpha

Why the years differ by source

Generation labels come from writers, researchers, and the media, and different sources draw the lines in different places — one list may start Millennials a year earlier or later than another. The table on this page is one widely used version, and it is the one this tool follows exactly. If you were born near the edge of a range, you may feel at home in either of the two neighbouring labels, and that is completely normal.

What your generation label does not say

A generation label is a fun shorthand, not a description of a person. People born in the same years can have very different lives, tastes, and opinions, and the label says nothing about who you are on your own. Use it as a conversation starter at a party or a family gathering — compare labels across the table, swap stories about growing up, and enjoy the differences. The year you were born is one small fact about you, and the rest of the story is yours to tell.
