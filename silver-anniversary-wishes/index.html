How to make a wish your own

Choose a wish that suits the couple, then weave in one real detail - how they met, a trip they still talk about, or the way they have supported each other through twenty-five years. A true memory makes a silver anniversary message shine.

Silver is the traditional symbol of a 25th anniversary, so a mention of silver in your message fits the occasion. Keep the wish warm and simple, and sign it with your name so the couple knows exactly who is celebrating with them.

Questions people ask

What is a silver anniversary?

A silver anniversary marks twenty-five years of marriage. Silver is the traditional symbol for this milestone, which is why so many 25th anniversary wishes mention it.

What should I write for a 25th anniversary?

Congratulate the couple on twenty-five years together, mention something you admire about their marriage, and wish them many more happy years. A personal memory makes the message special.

Is it okay to make a joke in a silver anniversary wish?

Yes, a gentle joke suits most couples, especially one about twenty-five years of shared life. Keep it kind and keep the congratulations at the heart of the message.

Can I use these wishes for my own silver anniversary?

Yes, several of the sweet wishes work well as messages to your own partner. Add a line about your own twenty-five years together to make it personal.

What is a good short silver anniversary wish?

A short wish that names the twenty-five years and the couple is perfect. The short wishes on this page are made for cards, texts, and social media posts.
