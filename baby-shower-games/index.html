Baby Shower Games

Choose from 16 complete baby shower games across Icebreakers, Guessing, Active and Keepsake, each with what you need, setup and how to play. Prize and timing tips help you plan a shower everyone enjoys.

All 16 games are family-friendly and gentle. Let guests join in at their own pace, and keep the active games light-hearted so everyone can play comfortably.

How to plan the games

Three to five games is plenty for a two-hour shower. Start with an icebreaker as guests arrive, play one or two guessing games while everyone settles in, add one active game for energy, and finish with a keepsake game that gives the mum-to-be something to take home. Leave time between games for food, chat and gift opening.

Prize and timing tips

  • Keep each game to ten minutes or less so the party keeps moving.
  • Small prizes work best: candles, chocolates, hand cream or a pretty notebook.
  • Have a prize ready for every game, plus one extra for a surprise tie.
  • Let the mum-to-be judge the creative games — it is her day.
  • Set up the keepsake games on a side table so guests can join in whenever they like.

Icebreakers

Baby Bingo

What you need: Printed bingo cards with baby items, pens.

Setup: Hand out a card to each guest as they arrive.

How to play: Guests mark off items as the mum-to-be opens her gifts. The first guest to complete a row shouts "Bingo!" and wins a small prize.

Who Knows Mummy Best?

What you need: Printed question sheets, pens.

Setup: Write ten questions about the mum-to-be, such as her favourite food or her childhood nickname.

How to play: Guests answer the questions, then the mum-to-be reads out the correct answers. The highest score wins.

Baby Name Race

What you need: Paper, pens, a timer.

Setup: Give each guest a sheet of paper and a pen.

How to play: Guests write a baby name for every letter of the alphabet in three minutes. The guest with the most names wins.

Guess the Baby Photo

What you need: Baby photos of the guests, numbered cards.

Setup: Ask guests to bring a baby photo and display them with numbers.

How to play: Guests match each photo to the guest they think it is. The most correct matches wins.

Guessing

How Big Is the Bump?

What you need: Ribbon or string, scissors.

Setup: Cut a length of ribbon for each guest.

How to play: Guests cut their ribbon to the length they think fits around the bump. The closest guess wins.

Guess the Baby Food

What you need: Six jars of baby food with labels removed, numbered spoons.

Setup: Number the jars and give each guest a tasting spoon.

How to play: Guests taste each jar and guess the flavour. The most correct guesses wins.

How Many Sweets in the Jar?

What you need: A jar of small sweets, slips of paper, pens.

Setup: Fill the jar and count the sweets in advance, then seal it.

How to play: Guests write down their guess. The closest guess wins the jar.

Guess the Due Date and Weight

What you need: A prediction chart, pens.

Setup: Draw a chart with columns for the arrival date and the weight.

How to play: Guests write their predictions on the chart. The closest to the real arrival wins after the baby is born.

Active

Baby Bottle Race

What you need: Baby bottles filled with juice, one per player.

Setup: Line the players up with a full bottle each.

How to play: On "go", players drink through the teat as fast as they can. The first empty bottle wins.

Nappy Change Challenge

What you need: Dolls, nappies, blindfolds.

Setup: Give each player a doll, a nappy and a blindfold.

How to play: Blindfolded players race to put the nappy on the doll. The neatest and fastest nappy wins.

Pass the Ring

What you need: A small ring and a straw for each player.

Setup: Split guests into teams and line them up.

How to play: Players pass the ring along the line using only the straws, with no hands. The first team to finish wins.

Baby Shower Charades

What you need: Slips of paper with baby-related words.

Setup: Write words like "rocking a baby" or "pushing a pram" on the slips.

How to play: Players act out the word without speaking while their team guesses. The team with the most correct guesses wins.

Keepsake

Wishes for Baby Cards

What you need: Cards, pens, a keepsake box.

Setup: Place the cards and pens on a table with the box.

How to play: Guests write a wish or a piece of advice for the baby. The cards are kept in the box for the family.

Decorate a Bib

What you need: Plain bibs, fabric markers.

Setup: Lay out the bibs and markers on a table.

How to play: Guests decorate a bib for the baby. The mum-to-be takes home a set of one-of-a-kind bibs.

Baby Time Capsule

What you need: A box, slips of paper, pens.

Setup: Place the box and paper on a table.

How to play: Guests write predictions and messages for the baby to read on a future birthday, then seal the box until then.

Onesie Decorating Station

What you need: Plain white onesies, fabric markers, stencils.

Setup: Set up a decorating table with the supplies.

How to play: Guests decorate a onesie for the baby. The parents take home a wardrobe of handmade outfits.

Questions people ask

How many games should we play at a baby shower?

Three to five games is plenty for a two-hour shower. Start with an icebreaker, add one or two guessing games, one active game, and finish with a keepsake game.

What prizes work best for baby shower games?

Small, simple prizes work best: candles, chocolates, hand cream or a pretty notebook. Have one prize per game, plus an extra for a surprise tie.

Do the games work for a mixed-age shower?

Yes. The icebreakers and guessing games suit every age, and the active games can be played gently. Let older guests judge or keep score if they prefer not to race.

What if guests arrive at different times?

Start with a game guests can join as they arrive, like Baby Bingo or the prediction chart, and save the timed games for when everyone is settled.

Can the keepsake games be set up in advance?

Yes. Set up the wishing cards, bib decorating or time capsule on a side table before the party starts, so guests can join in whenever they like.
