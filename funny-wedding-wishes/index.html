How to personalize your wish

A funny wedding wish works best when the joke lands and the warmth shows through. Pick a wish that matches the couple's humour, then add one personal line to make it yours.

  • Keep the joke kind — tease the situation, never the people.
  • Add a detail about the couple, like how they met or what makes them laugh together.
  • End on something sincere, so the laughter finishes with a warm feeling.

Questions people ask

What makes a funny wedding wish work?

A joke that is kind, a detail about the couple, and a warm landing. The best funny wishes make everyone smile first and feel something second.

How funny is too funny for a wedding card?

If the joke teases the situation rather than the people, it is safe. Avoid anything about past relationships, money, or anything the couple would not want read aloud.

How do I copy a wish from this page?

Tap the Copy button under any wish to save it to your clipboard, then paste it into a card, a message, or a wedding guest book note.

Can I use a funny wish in a speech?

Yes, one or two short lines are perfect for a speech. Keep them clean, keep them kind, and follow them with something sincere so the laughter ends on a warm note.

What if the couple is quite traditional?

Then choose the gentlest jokes on this page — the ones about shared blankets and takeaway debates — and pair them with a sincere line about their future together.
