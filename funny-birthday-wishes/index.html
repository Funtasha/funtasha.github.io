How to make it your own

A funny wish lands best when it fits the person you are sending it to. Try one of these before you send it:

  • Pick the joke that matches their sense of humour — gentle teasing for a close friend, a cake joke for anyone.
  • Add one true detail, like their favourite cake or the show they always stay up too late watching.
  • Pair a funny line with one warm sentence, so the joke arrives with the love attached.
These wishes are jokes, not roasts. Every line is written to be kind and good-natured, and it works best when the person reading it knows you are laughing with them, not at them.

Questions people ask

Are these funny birthday wishes safe to send to anyone?

Yes. Every line is kind and good-natured, and none of them mocks ageing, looks, or anything personal. If you are unsure, pick a cake joke — those suit everyone.

How long should a funny birthday message be?

One or two lines is plenty for a text, and two or three lines is plenty for a card. A short joke that fits the person beats a long one that could be for anyone.

Can I send these wishes by text message?

Yes. The short and silly lines are written to fit a text, and the copy button copies the whole wish so you can paste it straight into your message.

What if I want something sweet instead of funny?

Pair a funny wish with a warm line of your own, or visit the short birthday wishes page for sweet one-liners that mix well with a joke.

How do I make a funny birthday wish more personal?

Add one true detail — their favourite cake, their hobby, or a memory you both laugh about. The wish gives you the joke; the detail makes it theirs.
