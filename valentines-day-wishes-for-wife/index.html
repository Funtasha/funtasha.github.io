These wishes are starting points, not finished cards. A line lands best when it carries a detail only you know — a shared memory, the way she makes you smile, or the small habit you love most about her.

How to make it your own

A wish becomes yours the moment you add something only you could write. Try one of these before you send it:

  • Name a memory: the first date, a trip together, or the evening she made you laugh until you cried.
  • Mention something you love about her — a habit, a talent, or the way she looks after you.
  • Sign it the way you always do — a nickname or a family phrase beats a formal ending.

Questions people ask

What should I write in a Valentine's card for my wife?

Start with a wish from the romantic section, then add one detail only you know — a shared memory, a habit you love, or a hope for the year ahead. Two or three heartfelt sentences are plenty.

How do I write a romantic message without sounding cheesy?

Keep it honest and specific: name something real you love about her, and say it in your own words. One sincere sentence beats a page of borrowed poetry.

Can I send these wishes by text message?

Yes. The short wishes are written to fit a text, and the copy button copies the whole wish so you can paste it straight into your message.

What if I want something funny instead of romantic?

Use the funny section — every line is kind and good-natured. If she loves a joke, pair one funny wish with one sweet line.

How do I make a Valentine's wish more personal?

Add a shared memory, name something you love about her, and sign it the way you always do. The wish gives you the words; the personal detail makes them yours.
