How the team picker works

Press Pick a team for me, and the picker draws one team at random from the eighteen that have already qualified for the Women's World Cup 2027. Your team appears with its confederation and a fun fact, so you know a little about who you are supporting before the tournament even starts.

Every team is drawn once before any team is drawn again — the picker works through the whole deck of eighteen, like dealing cards, so you will never get the same team twice in a row. When all eighteen have been drawn, the deck shuffles itself and starts again. Press the button as many times as you like: for yourself, for your friends, or for a whole room full of undecided fans.

The 18 teams in the draw

The picker draws only from the eighteen teams that had already qualified as of mid-November 2026. From South America: Brazil, the hosts, plus Argentina and Colombia. From Europe: Denmark, France, Germany and Spain. From Asia: Australia, China PR, Japan, Korea DPR, Korea Republic and the Philippines. From Africa: Algeria, Cameroon, Malawi and Morocco. And from Oceania: New Zealand.

The other fourteen places at the tournament were still being decided when this list was made — seven through the European play-offs, four through the Concacaf championship, and three through a final inter-confederation play-off in February and March 2027. Those teams are not in the picker, because they had not qualified yet. The USA and England are among the teams still playing for their places, so neither appears in the draw.

Why your team might be a surprise

Most fans walk into a World Cup with a team already in mind — usually their own country, or the team they have always followed. The picker is for everyone else: the fan with no team, the newcomer who wants a reason to care, and the curious supporter who wants a second team to cheer for when their own is not playing.

A random draw often hands you a team you would never have chosen, and that is the point. Supporting Morocco because the picker said so, or following Malawi on their first ever World Cup, gives you a story to follow through the tournament. The best World Cup memories often come from the team you adopted by accident — the one whose matches you watched because you had a stake in them, however small.

Following your team to Brazil

Once you have your team, the tournament becomes yours to follow. The Women's World Cup 2027 is played in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027, with 32 teams playing 64 matches across eight host cities: Belo Horizonte, Brasilia, Fortaleza, Porto Alegre, Recife, Rio de Janeiro, Salvador and Sao Paulo. The final draw on 11 December 2026 will tell you which group your team is in and which cities they play in.

Until the draw, follow your team's build-up: their friendly matches, their squad news, and the stories around them. A team drawn at random in November can feel like your team by June, if you follow it with a little attention. And if your team reaches the knockout rounds, the matches matter in a way that no neutral game ever could.

Teams to know: the champions and the hosts

Three teams in the draw carry the biggest stories. Spain arrive as defending champions, having beaten England 1-0 in the 2023 final in Sydney. They play the quick, skilful football that won them the title, and they are the team every other side will be measured against. Brazil are the hosts, welcoming the tournament to South America for the first time, with all the pressure and all the joy that brings — and a home crowd that will fill the Maracana for the opening match on 24 June.

Japan won the tournament in 2011, beating the USA on penalties in Frankfurt in one of the great finals, and they remain one of the most organised and disciplined teams in the world. Germany won in 2003 and 2007 and have the pedigree of champions. If the picker hands you one of these four, you have drawn a team with a genuine chance of lifting the trophy on 25 July 2027.

First-timers and returners

Some of the best stories in the draw belong to the teams with the least history. Algeria and Malawi are playing at their first ever Women's World Cup, and everything they do in Brazil will be a first: first match, first goal, first point. Following a debut team means sharing in moments their fans will remember for the rest of their lives.

Others are coming back after a long wait. Korea DPR return to the World Cup for the first time since 2011. The Philippines made their debut in 2023 and won their first ever World Cup match. Morocco reached the round of 16 on their debut in 2023, and New Zealand won their first ever World Cup match in 2023 too. These are the teams that make a World Cup feel new, and the picker gives you as good a chance of drawing one of them as any other team in the deck.

Making match day fun

Once the tournament starts, your drawn team turns every match day into an occasion. Wear their colours if you have them, cook a dish from their country for the match, and learn one fact about them to share with whoever is watching with you — the picker gives you the first fact, and the rest you will pick up as you follow them.

If you drew your team with friends, make a small competition of it: whoever's team goes furthest wins, and whoever's team goes out first makes the snacks for the final. The bingo card generator on Funtasha can add to the fun on match day, and the quiz will test what you have learned along the way. A team picked at random, followed with enthusiasm, is as good as any team picked on purpose — sometimes better.
