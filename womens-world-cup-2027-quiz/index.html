What it asks about is what is already known — and there is plenty of that.

The road to Brazil 2027

The Women's World Cup 2027 is played in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. Thirty-two teams take part, playing 64 matches across eight host cities. The opening match is Brazil at the Maracana in Rio de Janeiro on 24 June, and the final is at the same stadium on 25 July. It is the first time the tournament has been staged in South America.

As of mid-November 2026, eighteen teams had already qualified: Brazil as hosts, Denmark, France, Germany and Spain from Europe, Argentina and Colombia from South America, Australia, China PR, Japan, Korea DPR, Korea Republic and the Philippines from Asia, Algeria, Cameroon, Malawi and Morocco from Africa, and New Zealand from Oceania. The other fourteen places were still being decided, through the European play-offs, the Concacaf championship and a final inter-confederation play-off in February and March 2027.

Past finals to know

The quiz leans on the last four finals, because they are the ones most fans remember — and the ones that shape the stories of 2027. In 2023, Spain beat England 1-0 in Sydney to win their first title, and they arrive in Brazil as defending champions. In 2019, the USA beat the Netherlands 2-0 in Lyon to win their fourth title. In 2015, the USA beat Japan 5-2 in Vancouver, in the highest-scoring final the tournament has seen. And in 2011, Japan beat the USA on penalties in Frankfurt, in one of the great finals.

Knowing these four finals gives you the spine of the modern women's game: the rise of Japan, the long dominance of the USA, and the arrival of Spain as the team to beat. If you can name all four winners and all four venues, you are already ahead of most quiz players — and you will enjoy the 2027 tournament all the more for knowing the history behind it.

The eight host cities

The eight host cities of 2027 are Belo Horizonte, Brasilia, Fortaleza, Porto Alegre, Recife, Rio de Janeiro, Salvador and Sao Paulo. Each has its own stadium: the Mineirao in Belo Horizonte, the Estadio Nacional in Brasilia, the Arena Castelao in Fortaleza, the Beira-Rio in Porto Alegre, the Arena de Pernambuco in Recife, the Maracana in Rio de Janeiro, the Arena Fonte Nova in Salvador, and the Arena Itaquera in Sao Paulo.

The Maracana carries the most history. It hosts the opening match, when Brazil play on 24 June, and it hosts the final on 25 July. Brazil's group matches take them to three cities: the opener at the Maracana, the second match in Sao Paulo on 29 June, and the third in Brasilia on 4 July. The other cities host group matches and knockout matches through the tournament, so wherever you are in Brazil in July 2027, the World Cup is never far away.

How to run a quiz night with friends

This quiz works just as well as a group game. Gather your friends, take it in turns to answer, and keep score on paper: one point for a right answer, nothing for a wrong one. Read the explanation after each question, because that is where the conversation starts — everyone has an opinion about the 2023 final, and the 2011 final still divides a room.

For a livelier night, split into teams and let each team discuss before answering. Give a bonus point to any team that can name the losing finalist as well as the winner. And finish with a prediction round: everyone names the team they think will lift the trophy on 25 July 2027, and the predictions go on the fridge until the final.
