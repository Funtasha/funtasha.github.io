How the countdown works

Mother's Day falls on the second Sunday of May. That is the rule, stated plainly: find the first Sunday of May, and Mother's Day is the Sunday after it. The date moves from year to year, but the rule never changes.

This page works the date out itself, in code. It finds the first Sunday of May in the current year, adds seven days to reach the second Sunday, and counts down to midnight at the start of that day in your local time. It updates every second while the page is open.

When Mother's Day arrives, the countdown shows that the day is here. Once the day is over, it rolls forward on its own to the second Sunday of the following May, so the page never needs updating and never goes out of date. The countdown runs entirely in your browser, using the clock on your own device.

Planning a Mother's Day surprise

The best Mother's Day surprises are the ones that show you were thinking of her, not just of the day. A few ideas:

  • Start the day with breakfast in bed, made with her favourite things.
  • Write a short handwritten letter about something she did that you still remember.
  • Cook her favourite meal, and let her sit down while you do the washing up.
  • Pick a photo from the year and have it printed, with a note on the back.
  • Spend time together doing what she loves, with no phones and no hurry.
The countdown runs on your device clock, so it is only as accurate as the time on your phone or computer. If your device clock is wrong, the countdown will be too.

Questions people ask

Mother's Day falls on the second Sunday of May. This countdown aims at midnight at the start of that day in your local time.

How is the date of Mother's Day worked out?

Find the first Sunday of May, then add seven days — the next Sunday is Mother's Day. This page does that sum itself, in code, every time it loads.

Does the countdown use my time zone?

Yes. It uses the clock on your own device, so the countdown is in your local time wherever you are.

What happens on Mother's Day itself?

The countdown shows that Mother's Day has arrived. Once the day is over, it rolls forward to the second Sunday of the following May on its own.

Does the countdown work without an internet connection?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, the countdown runs entirely in your browser from your device clock, with no connection needed.
