Two Truths and a Lie Generator

Two Truths and a Lie is a party game where one player reads three statements and everyone else guesses which one is the lie. This generator deals a fresh triple from 65 original sets, and you can reveal the lie with a tap.

Press "Deal a new triple" to begin.

These statements are ready-made lines for the game — pick ones that suit your group, and skip any that do not feel right for your players.

How to play

One player is the reader. They press the button, read the three statements out loud as if they were all true, and everyone else tries to spot the lie. Once every player has guessed, press the reveal button to see the answer.

Score one point for each player who guesses correctly, and give the reader a point if nobody guesses the lie — a well-hidden lie deserves a reward. Then pass the device to the next reader and deal a new triple.

The game works with as few as three players and as many as you can fit around a table. With a big group, split into teams and let each team discuss its guess before answering — the debates are half the fun.

The generator never repeats a triple until you have seen the whole deck, and you can filter by category if you want a round that is all food, all travel, or all animals.

Make your own triples

Once you have played a few rounds, try writing your own triples. The best ones mix the ordinary with the surprising, so the lie hides among everyday facts.

  • Start with two real facts. Pick things that are true but not obvious — a hobby, a small achievement, or a place you have been.
  • Make the lie believable. A lie that is too wild gives itself away. The best lies sound like something you might actually have done.
  • Keep it kind. Avoid statements that could embarrass anyone, including yourself — the game is about guessing, not confessing.
  • Vary the shape. If both truths are about food, make the lie about food too, so the odd one out is not obvious.
  • Read them flat. Say all three in the same tone, with the same smile — a grin at the wrong moment is the easiest tell.

Questions people ask

How do you play Two Truths and a Lie?

One player reads three statements — two true, one false — and the other players guess which one is the lie. Press the reveal button to check, then pass to the next reader.

How many people can play?

Three players is the minimum, and there is no real maximum. With a big group, split into teams and let each team agree on one guess.

What makes a good lie?

A good lie sounds like something the reader might actually have done. If it is too wild or too dull, it gives itself away — the best lies sit right next to the truth.

Can children play?

Yes. Every triple in the bank is family-safe, and the School and Animals categories are especially good for younger players.

How do I reveal the lie?

Press the Reveal the lie button under the triple. The lie is marked in the list, and you can deal a new triple for the next round.
