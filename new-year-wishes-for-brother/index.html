These wishes are starting points, not finished cards. A line lands best when it carries a detail only you know — a shared adventure, an inside joke, or the memory you both still laugh about.

How to make it your own

A wish becomes yours the moment you add something only you could write. Try one of these before you send it:

  • Name a memory: the game you played together, the trip you took, or the time you laughed until you cried.
  • Mention something you are looking forward to doing together, so the wish points at your brotherhood and no one else's.
  • Sign it the way you always do — a nickname or a private joke beats a formal ending.

Questions people ask

What should I write in a New Year message for my brother?

Start with a wish from the warm section, then add one detail only you two share — a memory, an inside joke, or something you are looking forward to doing together. Two or three heartfelt sentences are plenty.

How long should a New Year message for my brother be?

Two or three sentences is enough for a card, and one sentence is enough for a text. A short message that is personal beats a long one that could be for anyone.

Can I send these wishes by text message?

Yes. The short wishes are written to fit a text, and the copy button copies the whole wish so you can paste it straight into your message.

When is the best time to send a New Year wish?

Any time from the last days of the old year through the first days of the new one works well. A message sent a little early still feels thoughtful.

How do I make a New Year wish for my brother more personal?

Add a shared memory, mention something you did together this year, and sign it the way you always do. The wish gives you the words; the personal detail makes them yours.
