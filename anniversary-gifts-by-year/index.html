Anniversary Gifts by Year: What Gift for Which Year?

Enter an anniversary year from 1 to 60 to see its traditional and modern gift themes, plus one gift idea to get you started. Some years have no widely agreed theme, and the finder will tell you so honestly.

There is no single official list of anniversary gifts. Lists vary between sources, especially for years 11 to 14 and 16 to 19, which have no widely agreed theme. Use the themes below as a starting point, not a rule.

How the tradition grew

The oldest milestones in the tradition are the big ones: silver at 25 years, gold at 50, and diamond at 60. Those three were celebrated long before anyone made a list for the years in between. As anniversaries became a more common celebration, retailers filled in the gaps during the 1920s and 1930s, giving nearly every early year its own material. A second, modern list was drawn up in the United States in 1937, offering a different theme for many of the same years. That is why the table above has two columns, and why they agree on some years and differ on others.

Years with no agreed theme

Not every year has a theme. Years 11 to 14 and 16 to 19 have no widely agreed theme in either the traditional or the modern list, and the same is true of most other years that are not in the milestone table. That is not a gap in this page: it is how the lists are. For those years, the best guide is personal meaning. A gift tied to a shared memory, a favourite photo, or something made by hand will always say more than a material chosen from a list.

How to use the list

Treat the themes as a starting point, not a rule. The traditional and modern columns give you two directions to explore, and you are free to mix them, pick the one that suits your partner, or ignore both and choose something entirely your own. A small gift that fits the theme and carries a personal touch will always beat a grand gift that means nothing. If a year has no theme at all, that is a kind of freedom: the only rule left is to choose something that says you were thinking of them.

Questions people ask

What is the traditional gift for the 25th anniversary?

Silver is the traditional gift for the 25th anniversary, and it is the modern gift too.

What is the modern anniversary gift list?

The modern list was drawn up in the United States in 1937 as an alternative to the older traditional list, with a different theme for many years.

Why do some anniversary years have no gift theme?

There is no single official list, and lists vary between sources. Years 11 to 14 and 16 to 19 have no widely agreed theme in either list.

Do anniversary gift lists differ by country?

Yes. In the UK, the 4th anniversary is sometimes Fruit or Linen instead of Fruit and Flowers, and the 7th is sometimes Copper or Wool instead of Wool.

What should I give for a year with no agreed theme?

Choose any gift with personal meaning. Something tied to a shared memory, a favourite photo, or a handmade touch is always a good place to start.
