How to personalize your message

The most memorable engagement wish for a sister is personal. Mention a childhood memory, a quality you have always admired in her, or a moment that showed you how happy her fiancé makes her. Keep it warm and heartfelt, and add a gentle family joke if it suits you both. Send it as soon as you hear the news, while the excitement is fresh.

Questions people ask

What should I write to my sister on her engagement?

Tell her how happy you are for her, welcome her fiancé warmly, and share a memory that shows how much she means to you.

Should engagement wishes for a sister be funny or sweet?

A mix works beautifully — a heartfelt line plus a gentle family joke captures what sisters are all about.

How do I make an engagement wish for my sister personal?

Add a shared memory, mention something you admire about her, and say what you love about her fiancé.

When should I congratulate my sister on her engagement?

As soon as you hear the news — a sister's excitement is one of the first things she will want to share.

What should I say to my sister's fiancé?

Welcome them warmly, tell them you are delighted they make your sister happy, and say you look forward to knowing them better.
