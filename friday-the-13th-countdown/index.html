How the next one is found

Every month has a 13th, and the weekday of that 13th changes from month to month. When the 13th happens to fall on a Friday, we get a Friday the 13th. The countdown above checks the 13th of each coming month in turn and stops at the first one that is a Friday.

There is at least one Friday the 13th in every year, and some years have two or three. They cannot be far apart: because of the way the calendar works, the gap between one Friday the 13th and the next is never more than a few months, and sometimes two fall in consecutive months.

The target is midnight at the start of the day, in your local time, so the countdown reaches zero just as the Friday the 13th begins. Once it has passed, the countdown rolls forward to the next one.

Fun ways to mark the day

Friday the 13th is at its best when it is treated as a joke the calendar plays on us. A few light-hearted ways to enjoy it:

  • Host a movie night. Pick a gentle mystery or a spooky-but-silly film, with popcorn and thirteen snacks on the table.
  • Play the number. Serve thirteen of something — thirteen biscuits, thirteen candles, thirteen balloons — and see who notices first.
  • Share the stories. Every culture has its own lucky and unlucky numbers and days. Comparing them over dinner is a fun way to see how much of it is just tradition.
  • Do something lucky on purpose. A kind act, a compliment, or a small gift turns the day into a good-luck day by choice.
  • Keep it light. If someone in your family genuinely dislikes the date, a joke and a smile go further than teasing.

Questions people ask

What is Friday the 13th?

Friday the 13th is a date that is both a Friday and the 13th day of a month. It is a calendar coincidence, and the countdown on this page shows when the next one arrives.

How often does Friday the 13th happen?

There is at least one Friday the 13th every year, and some years have two or three. The gap between one and the next is never more than a few months.

Is Friday the 13th unlucky?

No. The date has no effect on anything — it is simply a Friday that happens to be the 13th. Some people enjoy treating it as a bit of fun, and that is all it is.

What time does the countdown end?

The countdown ends at midnight local time at the start of the Friday the 13th, so it reaches zero just as the day begins.

Why do people talk about it?

The number thirteen has been called unlucky in some traditions for a long time, and Friday has its own old stories. Put them together and you get a date people love to talk about — but it is only talk.
