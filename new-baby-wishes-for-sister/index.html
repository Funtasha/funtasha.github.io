These wishes are starting points, not finished messages. Add the baby's name, or a memory of growing up with your sister, and the wish becomes truly yours.

How to make a wish your own

Pick a wish that matches your relationship with your sister, then add the baby's name and a line from your shared childhood - the games you played, the secrets you kept, or the way she always looked after you. Sisters love messages that show you remember.

If you are now an auntie, say so in your message - it is a happy role, and your sister will love hearing it. A short wish suits a text, while a longer one fits a card tucked into a baby gift.

Questions people ask

What do I write to my sister who just had a baby?

Congratulate her, welcome your new niece or nephew, and tell her what you admire about her as a mum. A message that mentions your own bond as sisters is always special.

Should I mention that I am now an auntie?

Yes, it is a joyful part of the news. Saying you are proud to be an auntie makes your message warm and personal.

Is a funny wish okay for my sister?

Yes, sisters usually enjoy a joke, especially one about motherhood or your shared childhood. Keep it kind and keep the congratulations at the heart of it.

What if my sister had a difficult birth?

Keep your message gentle and focus on love and rest. A simple wish that says you are thinking of her and her baby is the kindest choice.

Can I use these wishes in a baby gift card?

Yes, every wish here works in a gift card. Add the baby's name and your own name to make it complete.
