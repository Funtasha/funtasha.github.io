How to personalize your message

Start with a wish from this page, then make it yours with a shared memory, a plan for the future, or a joke only the two of you understand. Mention something you hope to do together now that they have free time — a trip, a regular lunch, or a long walk. Friends love messages that look forward as well as back, so end with something to look forward to.

Questions people ask

What should I write to a friend who is retiring?

Congratulate them, say what you admire about their career, and wish them joy in the free time ahead — warm and cheerful suits a friend best.

How do I make a retirement message to a friend personal?

Mention plans you hope to make together, a shared memory from their working years, or a hobby you know they love.

Can retirement messages to friends be funny?

Yes — friends enjoy jokes about lie-ins, forgotten weekdays, and endless free time, as long as they stay kind.

Should I mention their career in the message?

A short mention of their hard work or achievements adds meaning, but the focus can stay on the happy years ahead.

When is the best time to send a retirement wish?

On or just before their last day is ideal, and a follow-up message a few weeks later is a lovely surprise.
