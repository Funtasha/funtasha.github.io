How to use them

Pick one line, not twenty. A page of a hundred quotes is a menu, not a meal. The line that helps you is the one you actually use, so choose one and give it a job.

Put it where you will see it. A lock screen is the best place — you look at it dozens of times a day, and the line sinks in without any effort. A wallpaper on your computer works the same way. A sticky note on a mirror works for the morning. A line in a journal works for the evening, when you write down what the day taught you.

Share it with someone who needs it. A motivational quote sent to a friend at the right moment is worth more than a hundred likes on a post. Send the line, add one sentence of your own — 'thinking of you' or 'you have got this' — and let the friend know the line was chosen for them, not forwarded at random.

Change the line to fit your life. If a quote says 'run' and you walk, change it to walk. If it says 'business' and you study, change it to study. A quote is a starting point, and the version that sounds like you is the one you will remember.

And remember the limit. A quote is a push, not a plan. It can start your morning, but it cannot do your work. Read the line, then do the thing. The line that matters most is the one you act on today.

Questions people ask

Can I use these motivational quotes for free?

Yes. Every quote on this page is free to copy and use in your own posts, messages, wallpapers, and journals. There is no sign-up and no charge. Lines marked Original were written for Funtasha, and the famous quotes are credited to their authors where the author is known.

Which quote is best for a hard day?

Pick a short one from the Never give up group — a line you can hold in your head without reading it twice. A long quote is hard to remember when you are tired, but a line like 'Tired is not finished' stays with you. Read it once in the morning and once in the afternoon.

Can I put a quote on my wallpaper?

Yes, and it is one of the best uses for these lines. A short one-liner fits a lock screen without covering the clock, and you see it dozens of times a day. Choose a line that pushes you toward one thing — starting, finishing, or standing up — rather than a line that only sounds nice.

Are these quotes real?

The famous quotes are credited to their authors where the author is known, and the wording follows the version most people know. Lines marked Original were written for this page. Lines marked Unknown are widely shared lines whose author is not known — we would rather say Unknown than invent a famous name.

How do I pick the right quote?

Match the line to the job. A morning line is for starting the day, a hustle line is for a work session, a never-give-up line is for a bad day, and a student line is for study. The right quote is the one that sounds like advice you would give yourself, not the grandest one on the page.

Can I share a quote with a friend?

Yes, please do. A quote sent at the right moment means more than a quote posted for everyone. Send the line, add one sentence of your own so your friend knows it was chosen for them, and keep it to one line — a long forward gets skipped, but a single line gets read.
