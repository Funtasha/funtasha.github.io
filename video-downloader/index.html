Yes — downloading videos here is completely free, and you don't need an account or sign-up.

Does the video download straight to my device?

Yes — tap Download video and the file saves straight to your device. Stream links (Vimeo and Dailymotion) open in a new tab where you can play them instead.

Why can't it find a video at my link?

The link may be private or need a login — only public videos work. A link to a photo, board or profile has no video file either. Platforms also change their pages sometimes, which can break a download until we catch up.

How long do I have to download a video?

Some video links expire after a while, so it's best to download the video as soon as you get it rather than saving the link for later.

No. Your link is used only to fetch the video, and nothing is stored or kept afterwards. The videos belong to their makers — please save them for personal use and ask before reposting.

Do I need to install an app?

No. It works in your browser on any phone or computer — paste a link, get the video.
