How to personalize your wish

A copied wish is a good start, but one personal detail turns it into a message only you could send. Pick a wish you like, then add a line about your brother's journey before you send it.

  • Mention the job itself — the role, the company, or the city he will be working in.
  • Add a shared memory, like the late nights he spent preparing or the day he told you he got the interview.
  • End with something forward-looking, such as lunch together after his first week.

Questions people ask

What is a good new-job wish for a brother?

A good wish is warm, specific, and short enough to read in one breath. Mention something real, like the long hours he put in or the role he landed, and end with encouragement for his first week.

Should I send a funny wish or a serious one?

It depends on your brother. If you two always joke around, a funny wish will land best. If this job meant a lot to him, a warm one shows you noticed how hard he worked.

How do I copy a wish from this page?

Tap the Copy button under any wish and it is saved to your clipboard, ready to paste into a message, a card, or a social post.

Can I change the words before sending?

Yes, and you should. Swap in his name, the job title, or a shared memory, and the wish will feel like it came from you rather than from a page.

When is the best time to send a new-job wish?

On the day he starts, or the evening before. A message waiting on his phone when he wakes up on his first day is a lovely way to begin a new chapter.
