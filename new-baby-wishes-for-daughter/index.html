New Baby Wishes for Daughter

Your daughter's new baby makes you a grandparent and your family a little brighter. This page has 25 original new-baby wishes for a daughter - sweet, funny and short - each with a copy button so you can share the one that fits her best.

Quick wishes for a text, a gift tag, or a card.

  • So proud of you, sweetheart. Welcome, little one!
  • A grandchild at last - congratulations, my dear daughter.
  • Welcome to the world, little one. We love you so much.

These wishes are starting points, not finished messages. Add your grandchild's name, or a memory of your daughter as a little girl, and the wish becomes truly yours.

How to make a wish your own

Choose a wish that sounds like you, then add your grandchild's name and a line about your daughter - the little girl she was, the woman she has become, or the mother you know she will be. Daughters treasure words from a parent on a day like this.

A short wish suits a text or a phone message, while a longer one fits a card or a letter. If you live nearby, offer practical help too - a cooked meal or an hour of babysitting means as much as any words.

Questions people ask

What do I write to my daughter who just had a baby?

Tell her how proud you are, welcome your grandchild by name, and say how much you love them both. A message from a parent on this day means the world.

Should I mention becoming a grandparent?

Yes, it is part of the joy. Saying how happy you are to be a grandparent makes your message warm and personal.

Is a funny wish okay for my daughter?

Yes, a gentle joke about motherhood or your own parenting days usually makes a new mum smile. Keep it loving and light.

What if my daughter is far away?

A heartfelt message travels well. Send a wish by text or card, and follow it with a call or video chat when she is ready.

Can I use these wishes for a son-in-law too?

Yes, you can address the wish to both your daughter and her partner. A warm message to the new parents together is always welcome.
