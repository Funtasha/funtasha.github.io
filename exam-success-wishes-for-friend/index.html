These wishes are starting points — your own encouragement, in your own words, will mean the most to a nervous friend, so add a detail only you know.

How to personalize your message

The most encouraging message is a personal one. Add a detail only you know — how hard they studied, a subject they mastered, or a moment you saw them keep going — and remind them you will be thinking of them on the day. Keep it short, warm, and calm, and avoid piling on pressure: your friend needs confidence, not more worry. A message sent the night before is perfect.

Questions people ask

What should I write to a friend before an exam?

Tell them you believe in them, remind them of the work they have done, and wish them a calm mind on the day — encouragement matters most.

How do I encourage a friend who is nervous about exams?

Remind them that one exam does not define them, that you are proud of their effort, and that you will be cheering for them.

Should exam wishes be funny or serious?

A mix works well — a warm encouraging line plus a light joke can ease nerves, as long as the joke stays kind.

When should I send an exam good-luck message?

The evening before or the morning of the exam is ideal, when nerves are highest and encouragement helps most.

What should I say after the exam is over?

Ask how it went, listen without judging, and remind them you are proud of their effort whatever the result.
