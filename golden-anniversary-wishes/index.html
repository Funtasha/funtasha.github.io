How to make a wish your own

Pick a wish that fits the couple's style, then add one real detail from their fifty years - the decade they married in, a family tradition they started, or a story their children still tell. Fifty years of memories give you plenty to choose from.

Gold is the traditional symbol of a 50th anniversary, so a mention of gold suits the occasion. If you are writing in a card, leave room for other family members to add their names - a golden anniversary is a whole-family celebration.

Questions people ask

What is a golden anniversary?

A golden anniversary marks fifty years of marriage. Gold is the traditional symbol for this milestone, which is why 50th anniversary wishes often mention it.

What should I write for a 50th anniversary?

Congratulate the couple on fifty years together, say what their marriage has meant to your family, and wish them continued happiness. A memory from their life together makes the message unforgettable.

Is humour okay in a golden anniversary wish?

Yes, a light joke about fifty years of marriage usually goes down well. Keep it affectionate and make sure the congratulations stay at the centre of the message.

Can I use these wishes for my parents' golden anniversary?

Yes, these wishes suit parents, grandparents, and any couple celebrating fifty years. Add a thank-you for what their marriage has given your family.

What is a good short golden anniversary wish?

A short wish that names the fifty years and congratulates the couple is ideal. The short wishes on this page work well in cards, texts, and speeches.
