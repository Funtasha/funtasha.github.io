Love Calculator: Check Your Name Match

Type in two names and this calculator gives you a playful love score from 0 to 100, plus a short message to match. The score is worked out from the letters of the names alone, so it is a party game, not a verdict — enjoy it, laugh about it, and take it with a pinch of salt.

This calculator is for fun only. The score comes from the letters of the two names and means nothing about a real relationship. Names do not decide compatibility — kindness, time and honesty do, and no number on a screen can measure those.

How the love score is made

The recipe is simple, and there is no secret to it. First, both names are tidied up: any extra spaces are trimmed away and every letter is made lowercase, so "Ana" and "ana" count as the same name. Then the two names are sorted alphabetically, which means the order you type them in makes no difference at all. Finally, the letters of the sorted names are fed into a simple character-code hash — each letter contributes its code number to a running total — and that total is mapped onto a score from 0 to 100.

Because the recipe never changes, the same two names always give the same score, today, tomorrow, or next year. There is no randomness in it and nothing is sent anywhere: the whole thing happens in your browser, and the names you type never leave the page.

The score comes with a short message that matches its band. Scores from 0 to 20 get a gentle nudge, scores from 81 to 100 get the biggest cheer, and everything in between gets a warm word of its own. Every message is written to be playful and kind, because a party game should never make anyone feel bad.

Fun ways to use your score

The love calculator works best as a shared joke. At a party, let guests pair up names and compare scores, then read the messages aloud and enjoy the reactions. On Valentine's Day, it makes a light-hearted addition to a card or a message — a little giggle to go with the real words. It also works as an icebreaker: two people who have just met can run their names through it and laugh about whatever number comes out.

Try it with friends, family members, or even fictional characters and celebrities. The calculator does not know who the names belong to, so any pair of names is fair game. The fun is in the comparing and the teasing, not in the number itself.

One thing to remember: a score is only ever a starting point for a laugh. If the number is high, enjoy the cheer. If it is low, enjoy the joke just as much. Either way, the real story between two people is written by what they do for each other, not by the letters in their names.

Questions people ask

Is the love score real?

No. The score comes only from the letters of the two names, run through a simple number hash. It says nothing about a real relationship, and it is meant purely as a bit of fun.

Why do the same two names always get the same score?

The calculator uses a fixed recipe: it lowercases both names, sorts them alphabetically, and turns their letters into a number. The same names always go through the same steps, so they always land on the same score.

Does it matter which name I type first?

No. The names are sorted alphabetically before the score is worked out, so swapping them around changes nothing.

What does a low score mean?

Nothing at all about the people involved. A low score only means the letters of those two names landed on a low number. Real compatibility is built on kindness, time and honesty, not on letters.

Can I use the love calculator at a party?

Yes, that is what it is for. Let guests pair up names and compare scores, and treat every result as a joke to laugh about together, never as a judgement.
