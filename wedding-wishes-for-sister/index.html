How to personalize your wish

Your sister will keep this card, so make it sound like you. Choose a wish that fits your relationship, then add a detail from your shared childhood or a hope for her future.

  • Mention a childhood memory, like a game you played or a secret you shared.
  • Welcome her partner by name — a short line to both of them means a lot.
  • End with a promise, such as being there for the big moments still to come.

Questions people ask

What is a good wedding wish for a sister?

A good wish mixes love with memory. Mention a moment from your childhood together, welcome her partner, and tell her how proud you are of the woman she has become.

Should I be funny or emotional in my wish?

A little of both is perfect for a sister. One gentle joke about growing up together, followed by something sincere, is a combination she will remember.

How do I copy a wish from this page?

Tap the Copy button under any wish to save it to your clipboard, then paste it into a card, a message, or a wedding guest book note.

Should I mention her partner in the wish?

Yes, if you can. A short line welcoming them by name makes the wish feel complete, and it means a lot to both of them.

What if I am giving a speech at the wedding?

Keep it short, warm, and kind. One funny memory, one sincere line about the couple, and a toast is all you need — the best speeches are the ones that end while everyone is still smiling.
