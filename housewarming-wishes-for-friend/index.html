How to personalize your message

Make your wish personal by adding a memory from the move, a room you helped with, or a meal you plan to share in their new kitchen. Friends love messages that are excited and a little playful, so do not be afraid to joke about visiting often or claiming the best seat. End with a real plan — a dinner, a film night, or a weekend visit — and your friend will smile every time they read it.

Questions people ask

What should I write to a friend in their new home?

Say how happy you are for them, mention something you love about the place, and promise a visit — friends value warmth and excitement.

How do I make a housewarming message to a friend personal?

Mention a memory from the move, a room you helped paint, or a meal you hope to share in their new kitchen.

Can housewarming messages to friends be funny?

Yes — jokes about claiming the best seat, eating their food, or visiting too often are perfect for close friends.

Should I offer help in my housewarming message?

A genuine offer to help unpack, cook, or lend a hand is always welcome, especially in the first busy weeks.

When should I visit a friend's new home?

Wait for an invitation or ask when suits them — the first weeks are busy, and a well-timed visit means more than a surprise one.
