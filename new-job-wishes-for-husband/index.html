How to personalize your wish

The best wish for your husband is one that sounds like you, not like a greetings card. Start with a wish from this page, then weave in a detail from your life together.

  • Mention what you admire most about how he got here — the patience, the late nights, or the quiet determination.
  • Add a small promise for his first week, like a favourite meal waiting at home on day one.
  • Keep it honest — a simple 'I am proud of you' in your own words beats a long speech.

Questions people ask

What is a good new-job wish for a husband?

A good wish mixes pride with affection. Mention what you admired about how he got the job, and tell him you will be cheering him on through the first week and beyond.

Should a wish for my husband be romantic or funny?

Either works, as long as it sounds like you. A romantic line suits a quiet moment together, while a funny one is perfect for a message he reads on his way to the office.

How do I copy a wish from this page?

Tap the Copy button under any wish and it is saved to your clipboard, ready to paste into a message, a card, or a social post.

Can I add my own words to a wish?

Yes, and it makes all the difference. Add his name, the job title, or a small promise for his first week, and the message becomes unmistakably yours.

When should I send the wish?

The evening before his first day is ideal — he will read it with his morning coffee and walk in knowing someone believes in him.
