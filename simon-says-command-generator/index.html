Simon Says Command Generator

Simon Says is a listening game where players move only when a command begins with the words Simon says. This generator deals from 63 original commands across Easy, Silly and Tricky levels, and flags each one for you.

Press "Deal a command" to begin.

The commands are written for mixed-age groups — skip any that do not suit your players, and keep the space clear so nobody bumps into anything.

How to play

One player is Simon — or, with this page, the page itself is Simon. Press the button to deal a command. If it starts with the words Simon says, everyone does it. If it does not, everyone must stay still. Anyone who moves when Simon did not say so is out, or loses a point.

The trick is speed. Deal commands quickly, one after another, and the players' ears start to race ahead of their brains — that is when the funny mistakes happen. Slow down for younger players and speed up when the group is warmed up.

The three levels let you tune the game: Easy commands suit the youngest players, Silly commands get the biggest laughs, and Tricky commands test coordination and memory. The generator never repeats a command until the whole deck is used.

Rules for mixed-age groups

Simon Says is at its best when everyone can join in, from the youngest child to the oldest grandparent. A few tweaks keep it fair.

  • Use the Easy level for young children. Simple commands like touching your nose or clapping keep them in the game.
  • Give a second chance. With mixed ages, let each player make one mistake before they are out — it keeps the game friendly.
  • Let children be Simon too. Reading the commands aloud is part of the fun, and it teaches turn-taking.
  • Keep the space clear. Commands that involve jumping or spinning need room — push the chairs back before you start.
  • End on a silly one. Finish with a Silly command that everyone can do together, so the game ends in laughter rather than an elimination.

Questions people ask

How do you play Simon Says?

Deal a command. If it starts with Simon says, everyone does it. If it does not, everyone stays still. Anyone who moves when Simon did not say so is out or loses a point.

What are the three levels?

Easy commands are simple actions for young players, Silly commands are playful and funny, and Tricky commands test coordination and memory. Choose a level or mix them all.

How often does Simon say it?

Most commands come from Simon, but not all of them — the generator decides each time, so players must listen to every command instead of guessing.

Will I get the same command twice?

Not until the deck is used up. The generator deals each command once, then shuffles a fresh deck and tells you it has done so.

Can young children play?

Yes. Use the Easy level, deal commands slowly, and give each player a second chance before they are out. The rules section above has more ideas for mixed-age groups.
