How to use them

Pick the line that sounds like you. A love quote works when it sounds like something you would actually say, not like a line from a film. If a quote feels too grand for your relationship, pick a shorter one. If a quote feels too small, pick a longer one. The right line is the one that makes you think of one specific person the moment you read it.

Put the line where it will be found. A card on a pillow, a text at lunchtime, a note in a lunchbox, a caption under a photo — the surprise matters as much as the words. A love line found by accident is remembered longer than a love line delivered on schedule.

Add one sentence of your own. The quote opens the message; your sentence makes it yours. 'This made me think of you' is enough. 'You laughed at this joke yesterday' is better. The line plus one true detail beats the line on its own every time.

Do not overuse them. A love quote every day becomes wallpaper. A love quote once a week, chosen with care, becomes a habit your partner looks forward to. Save the best lines for the moments that matter, and let the ordinary days carry ordinary words.

And remember: a quote is a beginning, not a substitute. The line says the feeling. The hug, the call, the time together — those prove it.

Questions people ask

Can I use these love quotes for free?

Yes. Every quote on this page is free to copy and use in your own cards, texts, messages, and captions. There is no sign-up and no charge. Lines marked Original were written for Funtasha, and the famous quotes are credited to their authors where the author is known.

Which love quote is best for a text message?

A short one — under fifteen words — that names one true thing about the person. The short love lines group is made for texts. Pick a line that sounds like you, add one sentence of your own, and send it when the person least expects it. A surprise text is remembered longer than a scheduled one.

How do I write a love quote in a card?

Put the quote in the middle of the card, and write one or two sentences of your own around it — one before, one after. The quote opens the feeling, and your words make it personal. Keep the handwriting clear, and do not fill every line: white space makes the words you did write feel bigger.

What is a good love quote for a long-distance relationship?

A line that names the distance honestly and says the waiting is worth it. The long-distance group has fifteen lines for couples apart — for the goodnight text, the video call, and the countdown to the next visit. Pair the line with a real plan: the next call, the next trip, the next date.

Can I use a love quote as a caption?

Yes. Short love lines work well under a couple photo, an anniversary picture, or a quiet moment together. Pick a line that matches the photo rather than the grandest one on the page, and keep the hashtags to three or five at the end. One true line beats a paragraph of poetry.
