How to personalize your wish

A parent's wedding wish carries more weight than any other, so let it sound like you. Choose a wish, then add a memory from her childhood or a hope you hold for her marriage.

  • Mention a small moment from when she was young — a bedtime story, a school play, a proud day.
  • Welcome her partner warmly and by name; it means the world to both of them.
  • End with something steady and true, like your pride in the woman she has become.

Questions people ask

What is a good wedding wish for a daughter?

A good wish from a parent mixes pride with love. Mention a memory from her childhood, welcome her partner, and tell her how proud you are of the woman she has become.

Should a parent's wish be emotional?

Yes, this is the moment for it. A parent's honest emotion is the most treasured part of any wedding card, so do not hold back — say what you feel, simply and truly.

How do I copy a wish from this page?

Tap the Copy button under any wish to save it to your clipboard, then paste it into a card, a message, or a wedding guest book note.

Should I mention her partner in the wish?

Yes, and warmly. A short line welcoming them by name tells your daughter that her whole family is gaining someone, not losing her.

What if I am speaking at the wedding?

Speak from the heart and keep it brief. One childhood memory, one line about the couple, and a blessing for their future is more than enough — the tears will take care of themselves.
