Setup guide for the host

Before the party, sit with the birthday person — or think back over what you know — and fill in the ten fact boxes above. Use the sample prompts as a starting point: favourite food, favourite colour, first pet's name, favourite film, favourite subject, dream holiday, favourite hobby, favourite song, childhood dream job, and favourite thing about birthdays. How pass-and-play works

One player holds the device and answers all ten questions, then passes it to the next player. After each player finishes, add their name to save their score on the scoreboard. The player with the highest score at the end knows the birthday person best. If two players tie, ask them a tie-breaker question of your own.

Tips for a fair quiz

  • Use facts the guests could reasonably know — the quiz is a celebration, not a test.
  • Keep facts short so they fit neatly on the answer buttons.
  • Read each question aloud so everyone can follow along.
  • Let the birthday person be the judge if a fact is disputed.

Questions people ask

How does the quiz builder work?

The host enters the birthday person's name and ten facts about them. The tool turns each fact into a multiple-choice question, mixing the true fact with three playful made-up options, and players answer on one device with scoring.

What if I only know a few facts about the birthday person?

The quiz needs all ten facts to give every player a full round. Use the sample prompts — favourite food, favourite colour, first pet's name — and ask the birthday person or their family to help fill in the gaps.

Could a made-up option accidentally be true?

The made-up options are deliberately silly — unicorns, dragons, robots and treasure chests — so they are clearly not real facts. They are there to make the quiz fun, not to trick anyone.

How does pass-and-play scoring work?

One player answers all ten questions, then you add their name to save their score on the scoreboard and pass the device to the next player. The highest score at the end wins.

Can I play the quiz again with the same facts?

Yes. Tap "Play again" to reshuffle the answer options and start a fresh round with the same ten facts. The scoreboard keeps earlier players' scores until you build a new quiz.
