Eid al-Fitr Wishes Generator

By the Funtasha team · Published 19 November 2026 · Updated 19 November 2026

Get a fresh Eid al-Fitr wish at one tap, chosen for family, friends, parents, children, or colleagues, and copy it ready to send. Every wish is written for this page, so yours will not read like everyone else's.

How the wishes generator works

Pick who the wish is for from the list, and press New wish. The generator chooses a wish written for that person: warm for family, playful for friends, grateful for parents, gentle for children, and polite for colleagues. Press the button again for another one, as many times as you like.

When you find a wish you like, press Copy wish and it goes straight to your clipboard, ready to paste into a message. The generator never repeats the same wish twice in a row, so every tap brings something fresh.

The wishes are grouped so that the tone always fits the reader. A wish for a parent sounds grateful, a wish for a child sounds cheerful, and a wish for a colleague sounds warm without being familiar. If you are unsure which group to pick, choose Anyone and let the generator surprise you.

When to send your Eid wish

Eid al-Fitr 2027 is expected on Tuesday 9 March 2027, at the end of Ramadan. The date depends on the sighting of the moon, so it may move by a day, but the festival is certain, and so is the pleasure of receiving a good wish on the morning itself.

The best time to send a wish is early on Eid morning, or the evening before, so it is waiting when the person wakes. If you have many people to write to, prepare the messages the night before and send them in the morning, one by one, with the person's name added at the start. A wish that arrives on time feels like a gift; a wish that arrives a week late feels like an afterthought.

Wishes for family

Family wishes are about home: the table, the laughter, the people who make the day feel like Eid. The family wishes on this page speak to the whole household at once, which makes them perfect for a family group chat or a card that everyone will read.

If you are writing to one person in the family, add their name at the start and a line of your own at the end. A wish that begins with a name and ends with a memory feels personal even when the middle came from a generator.

Wishes for friends

Friend wishes are lighter. They mention food, laughter, and the small pleasures of the day, because that is what friends share at Eid. A good friend wish makes the reader smile, and the ones on this page are written to do exactly that.

Friends who live far away appreciate a wish most of all. A short message on Eid morning tells them they were in your thoughts, even when you could not share the day. Send one early, before the day gets busy, and it will be waiting when they wake.

Wishes for parents

Parents deserve the warmest wishes of all. The parent wishes on this page are grateful ones: they thank mothers and fathers for the Eids they made special, and wish them health, peace, and long life. They are written to be read aloud, or sent as a message first thing in the morning.

If your parents are far away, a wish with a phone call means even more. Send the message, then ring them, and let them hear your voice. No generator can replace that, but a good wish is a fine way to begin.

Wishes for children

Children love Eid for the sweets, the new clothes, and the feeling that the whole day belongs to them. The wishes for children on this page are short, cheerful, and full of the things they love: treats, play, and surprises.

Read the wish to a young child, or write it on a card and tuck it beside their Eid gift. Older children enjoy receiving a message of their own, especially one that treats them as the star of the day, which, to their family, they are.

Wishes for colleagues and neighbours

Colleague wishes are warm but polite: they wish the person a joyful celebration and a restful break, without assuming too much about their life. They work for workmates, neighbours, teachers, and anyone you know well enough to greet but not well enough to tease.

A short Eid message to a colleague is a kind professional habit. It takes a moment to send, it is remembered far longer than most work messages, and it costs nothing. Send it on the day itself, or the evening before, when it will be read in a happy mood.

Writing your own Eid wish

A generator is a starting point, not a finishing line. The best Eid wishes have one line that only you could write: a memory, an inside joke, or a hope for the person you are writing to. Use a wish from this page as the frame, then add your own line at the end.

Keep it short and sincere. Three lines are plenty: a greeting, a warm thought, and a wish for the year ahead. If it sounds like you when you read it back, it is ready to send.

One more tip: read the wish once more before you send it, and check the name. A wish sent to the right person with the wrong name is the one mistake that no generator can fix, and it takes only a second to avoid.

These wishes are written for Eid al-Fitr. If you are writing to someone who does not celebrate Eid, a simple note of goodwill is kinder than a festival wish.

Questions people ask

How many wishes are there?

There are more than thirty wishes on this page, spread across five groups: family, friends, parents, children, and colleagues. Choosing Anyone draws from all of them at once.

Can I copy a wish to send it?

Yes. Press the Copy wish button under the wish and it goes to your clipboard, ready to paste into a message. If copy does not work in your browser, the page will tell you how to copy by hand.

Are the wishes free to use?

Yes. Every wish on this page is free to copy, send, and share. You can use them in messages, on cards, or in a family group chat.

What if I want a longer message?

Use a wish from the generator as a starting point and add a line of your own at the end: a memory, a hope, or a thank you. A short personal line makes any wish feel written just for its reader.

Do these wishes work for Eid al-Adha too?

Some of them do, since both festivals share the same warm spirit, but the wishes here are written for Eid al-Fitr, the festival at the end of Ramadan. For Eid al-Adha there is a separate wishes generator on Funtasha.

Does the generator work on a phone?

Yes. The generator runs entirely in your browser with no sign-up and no download, and it works on phones, tablets, and computers.
