Wedding Hashtag Generator: Hashtags From Your Names

Type in your first names, add a shared last name and a wedding year if you like, and this generator turns them into hashtag ideas in four styles: classic, joined, blended, and punny. Every idea has its own copy button, and the same names always give the same list.

This tool cannot check whether a hashtag is already in use. Search every finalist on Instagram and TikTok before printing it on signs or invitations — if someone else is already using it, pick another idea from your list or add your wedding year to make it yours.

How to pick a good wedding hashtag

The best wedding hashtags are short, easy to spell aloud, and easy for guests to type on a phone. Say your favourite out loud: if a guest could not spell it after hearing it once, it is probably too tricky. Short names help, and so does a clear shape — this generator capitalises the start of each word inside every hashtag, which makes a long tag much easier to read at a glance.

Keep a shortlist of three or four favourites rather than settling on the first idea. Try each one on a friend who has never seen it, and watch whether they read it back correctly. A tag that only makes sense once you explain it will not survive a busy wedding day. And remember that the hashtag is for your guests as much as for you — the easier it is to use, the more photos it will collect.

Where to put your hashtag

Put your hashtag where guests will actually see it: on the invitations, on a sign at the venue, and on your wedding website. A small card on each table works well too, and a mention in a toast reminds everyone to use it when the dancing starts. One hashtag used everywhere collects far more photos than three different ones scattered around the day.

It also helps to use the hashtag yourself in the run-up to the wedding, on any posts about the planning. That way, when guests search for it on the day, they find your photos first and know they have the right tag.

What if your favourite is taken?

Popular name combinations sometimes collide with another couple's hashtag, so always search your finalists on Instagram and TikTok before you print anything. If a tag is already full of someone else's photos, do not use it — your guests' pictures would be lost in a stranger's album.

The fix is usually simple: pick another idea from your list, or add your wedding year to the tag to make it yours. A year at the end of a hashtag is easy to remember and almost always makes it unique, which is why this generator offers year versions of most styles.

Questions people ask

Do I need a last name to use the generator?

No. The classic last-name style needs one, but the first-name joins, blended names and pun styles work with just two first names. Add a last name whenever you like to unlock the classic set.

Why do the same names always give the same hashtags?

The generator uses fixed templates, not random picks. The same names go through the same formulas every time, so the list never changes — you can come back to it later and find your favourites still there.
