Christmas Party Games

Choose from 16 complete Christmas party games for mixed-age groups across Kids, Family and All Together, each with setup and rules. Timing and prize tips help you plan a party everyone enjoys.

Every game here is alcohol-free and family-friendly, so the whole family can join in together. Let the youngest guest hand out the prizes — it is a lovely job for a child.

Planning your Christmas party games

Three to five games is plenty for a Christmas party. Start with a game for the children while the grown-ups arrive, play one or two family games after the meal, and finish with an all-together game that brings every age into the same circle. Leave room in the evening for food, music and conversation.

Timing and prize tips

  • Keep each game to ten or fifteen minutes so the party keeps its sparkle.
  • Small prizes suit the season: chocolates, small ornaments, or a festive notebook.
  • Have a prize for every game, plus one extra for a surprise tie.
  • Let the youngest guest hand out the prizes — it is a lovely job for a child.
  • Set up the wishes tree early so guests can add to it all evening.

Kids

Pin the Nose on Rudolph

Setup: Draw a large reindeer face on paper and cut out red noses.

Rules: Blindfolded players try to pin the nose in the right spot. The closest nose wins.

Christmas Stocking Hunt

Setup: Hide small stockings or treats around the room.

Rules: Children search for the hidden stockings. The child who finds the most wins a small prize.

Snowball Toss

Setup: Make soft snowballs from white socks or paper and place a bucket a few steps away.

Rules: Players toss the snowballs into the bucket. The most snowballs in wins.

Christmas Bingo

Setup: Print bingo cards with Christmas pictures.

Rules: The host calls out pictures and players mark their cards. The first full row wins.

Decorate the Tree Race

Setup: Give each team a small tree and a box of decorations.

Rules: Teams decorate their tree in three minutes. The best-decorated tree wins a vote.

Santa Says

Setup: One player is Santa and gives instructions.

Rules: Players only follow instructions that start with "Santa says". Anyone who moves without it is out.

Family

Christmas Trivia

Setup: Prepare 20 questions about Christmas traditions and songs.

Rules: Players answer in teams. The team with the most correct answers wins.

Gift Wrap Relay

Setup: Give each team a box, wrapping paper and ribbon.

Rules: Teams race to wrap the box neatly. The fastest neat wrap wins.

Christmas Charades

Setup: Write Christmas words and songs on slips of paper.

Rules: Players act them out while their team guesses. The most correct wins.

Guess the Christmas Carol

Setup: Prepare short clips of well-known carols.

Rules: Play a few seconds of each carol. Players write down the title. The most correct wins.

Ornament Guessing Jar

Setup: Fill a jar with small ornaments and count them in advance.

Rules: Players guess how many ornaments are in the jar. The closest guess wins.

All Together

White Elephant Gift Swap

Setup: Each guest brings a small wrapped gift.

Rules: Guests take turns choosing a gift or swapping with someone who already chose. The swap ends when everyone has a gift.

Christmas Scavenger Hunt

Setup: Hide Christmas-themed items around the house or garden.

Rules: Teams follow clues to find the items. The first team to find them all wins.

Holiday Pictionary

Setup: Prepare a list of Christmas words.

Rules: Players draw the word while their team guesses. The team with the most correct guesses wins.

Christmas Movie Quiz

Setup: Prepare questions about well-known Christmas films.

Rules: Players answer in teams. The team with the most correct answers wins.

New Year Wishes Tree

Setup: Set up a small tree with blank cards and pens.

Rules: Guests write a wish for the new year and hang it on the tree, then read them aloud together at the end of the party.

Questions people ask

How many games should we play at a Christmas party?

Three to five games is plenty. Start with a game for the children, play one or two family games after the meal, and finish with an all-together game for every age.

Do the games work for mixed-age groups?

Yes. The Kids games are simple enough for little ones, the Family games suit everyone, and the All Together games are made to bring every age into the same circle.

Are any of the games centred on alcohol?

No. Every game on this page is alcohol-free and family-friendly, so the whole family can join in together.

What prizes work best for Christmas party games?

Small seasonal prizes suit the occasion: chocolates, small ornaments, or a festive notebook. Have one prize per game, plus an extra for a surprise tie.

Can the games be played outdoors?

The scavenger hunt and snowball toss work well outdoors if the weather allows. Keep the rest indoors, where the food, music and warmth are.
