These wishes are starting points, not finished letters. The best anniversary message sounds like you, so add a detail only the two of you share before you send it.

How to make a wish your own

Choose the wish that matches how you usually speak to your wife, then replace one line with a memory only the two of you share - your first date, a holiday that went wrong in a funny way, or the song that always makes her smile. One true detail makes the whole message feel personal.

Think about where she will read it: a short wish suits a text or a note left on the mirror, while a romantic one deserves a card or a quiet moment together. Sign it with the name only you call her, and the message becomes unmistakably yours.

Questions people ask

What is a good anniversary wish for a wife?

A good anniversary wish for a wife is sincere and specific. Tell her what you admire about her, name a memory from your life together, and say what you look forward to next.

Should I write something romantic or funny for my wife?

Choose the tone she enjoys most. A romantic message is lovely for a special evening, and a funny one can be just as loving if humour is part of how you two connect.

How do I make an anniversary wish feel personal?

Add one detail that only applies to the two of you - a place, a joke, a habit, or a moment from the past year. That single detail makes any wish feel written just for her.

Can I use one of these wishes in a card?

Yes, every wish here works in a card. Copy it, write it out by hand if you can, and add a line of your own at the end.

What if my wife prefers short messages?

The short wishes on this page are made for that. A few warm words, honestly meant, can say everything she needs to hear.
