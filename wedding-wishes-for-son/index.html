How to personalize your wish

A parent's wedding wish is one he will keep for years, so make it sound like you. Choose a wish, then add a memory from his childhood or a hope you hold for his marriage.

  • Mention a small moment from when he was young — a game he loved, a proud day, a funny habit.
  • Welcome his partner warmly and by name; it means the world to both of them.
  • End with something steady and true, like your pride in the man he has become.

Questions people ask

What is a good wedding wish for a son?

A good wish from a parent mixes pride with love. Mention a memory from his childhood, welcome his partner, and tell him how proud you are of the man he has become.

How do I keep a parent's wish from sounding formal?

Write the way you talk at home. A parent's wish lands best when it sounds like a conversation, not a speech — short sentences, honest words, and one memory only you would mention.

How do I copy a wish from this page?

Tap the Copy button under any wish to save it to your clipboard, then paste it into a card, a message, or a wedding guest book note.

Should I mention his partner in the wish?

Yes, and warmly. A short line welcoming them by name tells your son that his whole family is gaining someone, not losing him.

What if I cry while giving the speech?

Then you cry, and everyone will love you for it. A parent's tears at a wedding are never embarrassing — they are the proof of how much this day means. Keep a tissue handy and carry on.
