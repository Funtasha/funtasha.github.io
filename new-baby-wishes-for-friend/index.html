New Baby Wishes for a Friend

A friend's new baby is a joyful moment, and the right words make it even sweeter. This page has 25 original new-baby wishes for a friend - sweet, funny and short - each with a copy button so you can send the one that fits your friendship best.

Sweet new-baby wishes for a friend

Warm wishes for a card, a gift, or a message.

  • Congratulations on your beautiful new baby! I cannot wait to watch this little one grow and to be there for every tiny milestone along the way.
  • Welcome to the world, little one - and welcome to the wildest, most wonderful adventure of your life, my friend. You are going to be an amazing parent.
  • I am so happy for you! Your baby is lucky to have a parent with your kind heart, your patience, and your wonderful laugh.
  • Congratulations, my friend! May your days be filled with tiny fingers, sleepy smiles, and a love bigger than you ever imagined.
  • Your little one has arrived, and my heart is bursting with joy for you. Congratulations on the most beautiful new chapter of your life.
  • Welcome to parenthood, my friend - the sleepless nights are real, but so is the love, and it is worth every single yawn. Congratulations!
  • Congratulations on your new baby! I already love this little person, and I cannot wait to spoil them every chance I get.
  • Your baby is here, and the world just got a little brighter. Congratulations, my dear friend - you were made for this.
  • So thrilled for you and your growing family! May your home be filled with laughter, love, and the sweet smell of baby clothes fresh from the wash.

  • Congratulations on the new baby! I have already started a savings account for the therapy you will need after the teenage years.

  • Your baby is adorable - clearly they take after you and not your partner. Congratulations, my friend!

Short new-baby wishes for a friend

Quick wishes for a text, a gift tag, or a card.

  • Congratulations on your beautiful new baby!
  • So happy for you, my friend. Welcome, little one!
  • A new baby, a new adventure. Congratulations!
  • Welcome to the world, little one. We love you already.
  • Congratulations - you are going to be a wonderful parent.
  • So much love for you and your new baby.
  • Hello, little one! Congratulations, my friend.
  • Your family just got sweeter. Congratulations!
These wishes are starting points, not finished messages. Add the baby's name if you know it, or mention something you love about your friend, and the wish becomes personal.

How to make a wish your own

Choose the wish that sounds like you, then add the baby's name and one line about your friend - how long you have known each other, or what you admire about them as a new parent. New parents remember who took the time to write something real.

A short wish suits a text or a social media comment, while a longer one fits a card or a gift tag. If you are close to the family, offer something practical too - a meal, a nap shift, or an afternoon of babysitting once they are ready.

Questions people ask

What do I write to a friend who just had a baby?

Congratulate them warmly, welcome the baby by name if you know it, and say something kind about them as a parent. A short, sincere message is always welcome.

Is a funny new-baby wish okay?

Yes, if your friend enjoys humour. A light joke about sleep or nappies usually makes new parents smile, as long as the congratulations come first.

Should I mention the baby's name in my wish?

Yes, if you know it. Using the baby's name makes any wish feel personal and shows you were paying attention.

What if I do not know the baby's name yet?

Use a general wish from this page and add it later. A warm message sent promptly means more than a perfect message sent late.

Can I send a new-baby wish by text?

Yes, the short wishes are made for texts. A quick, warm message in the first days means a lot to new parents.
