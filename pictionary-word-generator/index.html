Pictionary Word Generator

Pictionary is a drawing game where one player sketches a word while everyone else guesses what it is. This generator deals from 130 original prompts across five categories, with a difficulty filter, a timer and no repeats.

How to play

Split into two teams and take turns drawing. The artist presses the button, sees a prompt, and has one minute — the timer on this page — to draw it while their team shouts out guesses. No letters, no numbers, and no talking from the artist: the drawing has to do all the work.

If the team guesses correctly before the timer ends, they score a point. If not, the other team gets one guess for a steal, and then the pencil passes to the next artist. The first team to an agreed score wins, or simply play until everyone has drawn twice.

Use the filters to set the mood: Easy prompts suit younger players and quick warm-up rounds, while Hard prompts make for long, funny games with confident artists. The generator never repeats a prompt until the whole deck is used, so every round feels fresh.

Drawing tips

You do not need to be good at drawing to be good at Pictionary — you need to be quick and clear. A few habits help more than talent.

  • Draw the big shape first. A circle for a head or a long line for a road gives your team something to guess from in the first seconds.
  • Add one clue at a time. If your team is stuck, add a detail that narrows it down — a fin for a fish, a handle for a cup.
  • Use the scene. Drawing a sun, a wave or a plate around your subject can say as much as the subject itself.
  • Keep moving. A fast, rough sketch beats a slow, perfect one — the timer does not wait for shading.
  • Watch your team. If they are close, a small nod or a pointed finger at part of the drawing can steer them home without a word.

Questions people ask

How do you play Pictionary?

One player draws a prompt while their team guesses what it is, with no letters, numbers or talking from the artist. Guess correctly before the timer ends to score a point.

How long is the timer?

The timer runs for one minute, which is the classic Pictionary round. You can pause it or reset it at any time with the buttons under the timer.

Can I filter the prompts?

Yes. Choose a category — Animals, Objects, Actions, Food or Places — and a difficulty — Easy, Medium or Hard — and the generator draws only from prompts that match both.

Will I get the same prompt twice?

Not until the deck is used up. The generator deals each matching prompt once, then shuffles a fresh deck and tells you it has done so.

What do I need to play?

Paper and pencils, or a whiteboard, and at least four players split into two teams. Everything else — the prompts and the timer — is on this page.
