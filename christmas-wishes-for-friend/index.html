These wishes are starting points, not finished cards. A line lands best when it carries a detail only you know — a shared memory, an inside joke, or the moment that made your friendship what it is.

How to make it your own

A wish becomes yours the moment you add something only you could write. Try one of these before you send it:

  • Name a memory: the trip you took together, the late-night call, or the time you laughed until you cried.
  • Mention something you did together this year, so the wish fits your friendship and no one else's.
  • Sign it the way you always do — a nickname or a family phrase beats a formal ending.

Questions people ask

What should I write in a Christmas card for a friend?

Start with a wish from the warm section, then add one detail only you two share — a memory, an inside joke, or something you are looking forward to doing together. Two or three heartfelt sentences are plenty.

How long should a Christmas message be?

Two or three sentences is enough for a card, and one sentence is enough for a text. A short message that is personal beats a long one that could be for anyone.

Can I send these wishes by text message?

Yes. The short wishes are written to fit a text, and the copy button copies the whole wish so you can paste it straight into your message.

What if my friend does not celebrate Christmas?

Choose a warm wish that focuses on friendship and the season rather than the holiday itself, or send a simple note saying you are thinking of them. The thought matters more than the occasion.

How do I make a Christmas wish more personal?

Add a shared memory, mention something you did together this year, and sign it the way you always do. The wish gives you the words; the personal detail makes them yours.
