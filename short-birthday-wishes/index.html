How to make it your own

A short wish becomes yours with one small addition. Try one of these before you send it:

  • Add their name at the start — a short wish with a name feels personal at once.
  • Add one detail, like their favourite cake or the plan you share for the day.
  • Sign it the way you always do, so the wish sounds like you.
These wishes are short by design, so they suit texts, cards, and social media messages. If you want a longer message, use one as an opening line and add a sentence or two of your own.

Questions people ask

How short should a birthday wish be?

One or two lines is enough for a text or a social media message. A short wish that names the person feels warmer than a long message that could be for anyone.

Can I send these wishes by text message?

Yes. Every wish here is one or two lines, and the copy button copies the whole wish so you can paste it straight into your message.

What if I want something funny instead of sweet?

Use the funny section — every line is kind and good-natured. If you are unsure, the simple section suits everyone.

How do I make a short birthday wish more personal?

Add their name and one detail, like their favourite cake or a shared plan. The wish gives you the words; the detail makes them yours.

Are short wishes okay for a card?

Yes. Write one short wish at the top of the card, then add a sentence or two of your own below it. The card feels personal without needing a long message.
