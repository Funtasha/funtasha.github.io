How to make it your own

A wish becomes yours the moment you add something only you could write. Try one of these before you send it:

  • Name a memory: your first date, a trip you took together, or the joke that still makes you both laugh.
  • Mention something he loves, so the wish fits him and no one else.
  • Sign it the way you always do — a pet name or a private phrase beats a formal ending.
These wishes are starting points, not finished cards. A line lands best when it carries a detail only you know — a shared memory, his favourite hobby, or the moment you knew he was the one.

Questions people ask

What should I write in a birthday card for my boyfriend?

Start with a wish from the romantic section, then add one detail only you know — a memory, an inside joke, or a hope for the year ahead. Two or three heartfelt sentences are plenty.

How long should a birthday message for a boyfriend be?

Two or three sentences is enough for a card, and one sentence is enough for a text. A short message that is personal beats a long one that could be for anyone.

Can I send these wishes by text message?

Yes. The short wishes are written to fit a text, and the copy button copies the whole wish so you can paste it straight into your message.

What if I want something funny instead of romantic?

Use the funny section — every line is kind and good-natured. If your boyfriend loves a joke, pair one funny wish with one romantic line.

How do I make a birthday wish for my boyfriend more personal?

Add a shared memory, name something he loves, and sign it the way you always do. The wish gives you the words; the personal detail makes them yours.
