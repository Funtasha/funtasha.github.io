How to personalize your message

Start with a wish from this page, then add one detail from your time working together — a project you shared, a habit of theirs you admired, or a moment that still makes you smile. Mention their name, and if the message is for a leaving card, keep it to two or three sentences so there is room for everyone. A sincere line about what they brought to the team means more than any formal phrase.

Questions people ask

What makes a good retirement wish for a colleague?

Thank them for something real they did, mention a quality you admired, and wish them well in the life ahead — keep it warm and sincere.

Should a retirement message to a colleague be funny?

A light joke works well if you know them, especially about lie-ins or escaping meetings — just keep it kind and never about their age.

How long should a retirement message be?

Two or three sentences is plenty for a card or a message — a longer note suits a farewell speech or a leaving card with space to fill.

What if I did not know the colleague well?

Keep it simple and respectful: congratulate them, thank them for their work, and wish them a happy retirement — that is always welcome.

Can I send a retirement wish after their last day?

Yes — a message in their first week of retirement often means even more, when the quiet mornings have just begun.
