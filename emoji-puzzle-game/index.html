Emoji Puzzle Game: Guess the Phrase

Each puzzle in this game is a row of pictures that stands for an everyday phrase, and your job is to work out what it says. Guess, ask for a hint, or reveal the answer, and your score keeps track as you play.

The puzzles use everyday phrases and sayings — there are no brand names, no celebrities and nothing rude, so the whole family can play.

How to play

Look at the row of pictures and work out which everyday phrase they stand for. The pictures can stand for a whole word, a sound, or an idea — a bee might mean the word bee, the letter B, or the idea of being busy. Type your guess and press the check button to see if you are right.

Stuck? Press the hint button for a gentle clue, or the reveal button to see the answer — revealing counts as a try in your score, so it is worth guessing first. Press the next button to move on to a fresh puzzle. The game deals each of the 44 puzzles once before shuffling a new deck.

Your score shows how many puzzles you solved out of how many you tried. A revealed answer counts as tried but not correct, so the score rewards honest guessing over peeking.

Tips for solving picture puzzles

Picture puzzles look mysterious until you learn the tricks. These habits will carry you through most of them.

  • Say the pictures out loud. Hearing the words often reveals a rhyme, a sound-alike, or a familiar saying.
  • Think of sayings first. Most puzzles hide a common phrase or proverb, so run through the sayings you know that fit the pictures.
  • Let one picture be a sound. A bee can be B, a leaf can be leave, and a sun can be son — sounds matter as much as meanings.
  • Use the hint early. A hint is not a failure — it often gives you the theme, and the answer follows on its own.
  • Play together. Two heads spot the answer faster than one, and the debates over what a picture means are half the fun.

Questions people ask

How do emoji puzzles work?

Each puzzle is a row of pictures that stands for an everyday phrase. The pictures can mean a word, a sound or an idea — say them out loud and the phrase often appears.

How do I guess an answer?

Type your guess in the box and press Check my guess. Small differences in capital letters and punctuation do not matter — the game compares the words.

What happens if I reveal the answer?

The answer is shown and the puzzle counts as tried but not correct in your score. Hints are free, so try a hint before you reveal.

How is the score counted?

Your score shows how many puzzles you solved out of how many you tried. A puzzle you solve counts as correct; a puzzle you reveal counts as tried only.

Are the puzzles suitable for children?

Yes. Every puzzle uses an everyday phrase, with no brand names, no celebrities and nothing rude. Younger players may enjoy playing with an adult for the trickier sayings.
