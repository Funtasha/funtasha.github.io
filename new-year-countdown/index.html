How the countdown works

The countdown aims at January 1 at midnight, 00:00, in your own local time. It counts down in days, hours, minutes and seconds, and it updates every second while the page is open, so you can watch the final seconds of the year go by.

When January 1 arrives, the countdown shows that the new year is here. Once the day is over, it rolls forward on its own to the next January 1, so the page never needs updating and never goes out of date.

The countdown runs entirely in your browser, using the clock on your own device. It does not need an internet connection once the page has loaded, and it does not send anything anywhere.

New Year Eve planning pointers

A good New Year Eve is mostly about small plans made early. A few pointers for a calm, happy evening:

  • Decide where you will be at midnight, and who you want beside you when the clock turns.
  • Plan the evening meal in advance, so the last hours are for company, not cooking.
  • Charge your phone before the evening, so the midnight photos are not cut short.
  • Prepare a short toast, even just one sentence about the year gone by.
  • Plan how everyone is getting home before the evening starts, so nobody has to decide late at night.
The countdown runs on your device clock, so it is only as accurate as the time on your phone or computer. If your device clock is wrong, the countdown will be too.

Questions people ask

When is the new year?

The new year begins on January 1. This countdown aims at midnight at the start of January 1 in your local time.

Does the countdown use my time zone?

Yes. It uses the clock on your own device, so the countdown is in your local time wherever you are.

What happens at midnight on New Year Eve?

The countdown shows that the new year has arrived. Once January 1 is over, it rolls forward to the next January 1 on its own.

Does the countdown work without an internet connection?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, the countdown runs entirely in your browser from your device clock, with no connection needed.

Why does this countdown show a different number from another one?

Countdowns can differ if they use different time zones or different device clocks. This one follows your own device clock in your local time.
