Newlywed Game Questions

Draw from 52 original newlywed game questions across How You Met, Daily Life, Favorites and Firsts, with how-to-play rules and scoring for showers and parties. Every question is warm and family-safe.

Every question here is warm and family-friendly. The game is about celebrating how well a couple knows each other — mismatched answers are part of the fun, never a test.

How to play the newlywed game

Give each partner a small whiteboard, a notepad, or a set of answer cards. The host reads a question, both partners write their answers without peeking, and then they reveal them together. A matching pair scores a point. Play ten to fifteen questions for a shower or party, and let the couple with the most matches take the title.

With one couple, the game is a sweet look at how well they know each other. With several couples, it becomes a friendly contest — keep a scoreboard and crown the winning couple at the end. Either way, the mismatched answers are the best part.

Scoring ideas

  • One point for each matching answer, with the highest score winning.
  • Double points for the Favorites round, where guessing is hardest.
  • A bonus point if both partners give the exact same wording.
  • For showers, let the guests guess the couple's answers too and score alongside them.

Questions people ask

How does the newlywed game work?

Both partners answer the same question separately, then reveal their answers together. A matching pair scores a point. Play ten to fifteen questions and crown the couple with the most matches.

How many couples can play?

One couple makes a lovely shower game, and several couples make a friendly contest. With more than one couple, keep a scoreboard and compare totals at the end.

What do we need to play?

A way for each partner to write an answer without peeking — small whiteboards, notepads or answer cards — plus a host to read the questions and keep score.

Are the questions family-safe?

Yes. All 52 questions are warm and family-friendly, covering how the couple met, daily life, favourites and firsts, with nothing embarrassing.

Can guests join in too?

Yes. At a shower, hand the guests answer cards and let them guess the couple's answers alongside them. The guest with the most correct guesses wins a small prize.
