Magic 8 Ball: Ask a Yes-or-No Question

Type a yes-or-no question, shake the ball, and read one of the 20 classic answers. The ball is a toy: it predicts nothing, and every answer is just for fun.

Ask a question, then shake the ball.

The ball predicts nothing and the answer is for fun only. The ball does not read your question — every shake is an equal random pick from the same 20 answers, so the same question can get a different answer next time.

How to play

Think of a question with a yes or no answer, type it into the box, and press Shake the ball. The ball rattles, the window clears, and one of the 20 classic answers appears. Ask the same question again and you may get a completely different answer — that is part of the fun.

The ball is a natural party game: pass it around the table and let everyone ask one question, use it to settle small decisions like which film to watch, or keep it on the side at a birthday party for guests to try. The toy dates to the mid-20th century.

What the 20 answers mean

Every shake draws one answer from the same set of 20. Ten of them are affirmative, five are non-committal, and five are negative, so a shake lands on a yes about 50% of the time, a non-committal answer about 25%, and a no about 25% — that is how the toy was made.

  • Affirmative (10): It is certain. It is decidedly so. Without a doubt. Yes definitely. You may rely on it. As I see it, yes. Most likely. Outlook good. Yes. Signs point to yes.
  • Non-committal (5): Reply hazy, try again. Ask again later. Better not tell you now. Cannot predict now. Concentrate and ask again.
  • Negative (5): Don't count on it. My reply is no. My sources say no. Outlook not so good. Very doubtful.

The honest limit

The ball cannot see the future and it cannot read your mind. It does not look at your question at all — it simply picks one of the 20 answers at random, so a yes means nothing more than a lucky draw. Enjoy the answer as a joke or a talking point, and never let it decide anything that matters.

Questions people ask

Does the ball really answer my question?

No. The ball does not read your question at all. Every shake picks one of the 20 classic answers at random, so the answer has no connection to what you asked.

Why do I get a different answer to the same question?

Every shake is a fresh random pick from the same 20 answers. Nothing is remembered between shakes, so asking twice can give two different answers.

What are the odds of a yes answer?

10 of the 20 answers are affirmative, so a shake lands on a yes about 50% of the time. A non-committal answer comes up about 25% of the time, and a no about 25%.

Can I ask any kind of question?

The ball only gives yes, no, or maybe-style answers, so questions with a yes-or-no answer work best. Open questions still get an answer, but it will not make much sense.

Is the Magic 8 Ball real fortune-telling?

No. It is a toy, and the answers are for fun only. The ball predicts nothing, and no answer should be taken as advice.
