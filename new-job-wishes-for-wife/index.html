How to personalize your wish

A wish lands best when it carries a detail only you would know. Choose a wish that fits your wife's sense of humour, then add one line that is unmistakably yours.

  • Mention the role or the moment she told you the news — the phone call, the grin, the happy dance.
  • Name a strength you have watched her use to get here, like her patience or her sharp thinking.
  • End with a small plan, such as a celebration dinner after her first week.

Questions people ask

What is a good new-job wish for a wife?

A good wish celebrates her talent and her effort. Mention the role she earned or the hard work behind it, and tell her how proud you are.

What if my wife is nervous about the new job?

Then lead with reassurance. Remind her of a challenge she has already handled well, and tell her the first week is about learning names and finding the good coffee, not about being perfect.

How do I copy a wish from this page?

Tap the Copy button under any wish and it is saved to your clipboard, ready to paste into a message, a card, or a social post.

Can I add my own words to a wish?

Yes, and it makes all the difference. Add her name, the job title, or a small plan to celebrate her first week, and the message becomes unmistakably yours.

When should I send the wish?

The evening before her first day is ideal — she will read it with her morning coffee and walk in knowing someone believes in her.
