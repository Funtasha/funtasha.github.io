Friendship Calculator: Check Your Name Match

Type two names and this calculator gives you a playful friendship score from 0 to 100, with a kind message to match. The score comes from the letters in the names only, so the same pair always gets the same result, and it is for fun — it means nothing about a real friendship.

How the score is worked out

Type two names and the calculator turns the letters into a score. It adds up the character codes of the letters in both names, multiplies the total by 31, and takes the remainder when divided by 101 — so the score always lands between 0 and 100. The two names are sorted before the maths runs, which means the order you type them in makes no difference: Maya and Jon get the same score as Jon and Maya. The same pair of names always gives the same score, every time you try it.

What the score means

The score comes with a kind, playful message band. High scores get warm words about a strong bond, and lower scores get gentle encouragement — every friendship starts somewhere, and a low number is just an invitation to plan a coffee or a long chat. The bands are written to be fun for everyone, never mean, so you can share your result with the other person without worrying.

For fun only: the score comes from the letters in the two names and nothing else. It means nothing about a real friendship, predicts nothing, and should never be taken seriously.

Fun ways to use your result

Try the calculator with your oldest friend, your newest friend, or a family member, and compare the scores over a cup of tea. It makes a light party game too: everyone checks their score with the birthday person, and the highest pair wins a small cheer. Whatever the number says, the real test of a friendship is the time you spend together — this page just gives you a playful excuse to talk about it.

Can I use names with spaces or unusual letters?

Yes — spaces and characters that are not letters are ignored, and the score is worked out from the letters that remain.
