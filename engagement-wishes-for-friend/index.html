How to personalize your message

The most memorable engagement wish is personal. Mention a moment that showed you how right they are for each other, a quality you admire in your friend, or a hope you hold for their future together. Keep it joyful and warm, and add a light joke only if it suits your friendship. Send it as soon as you hear the news.

Questions people ask

What should I write to a friend who just got engaged?

Congratulate them warmly, say how happy you are for them both, and mention something you love about them as a couple.

Should engagement wishes for a friend be funny or romantic?

Either works — a sweet message suits a heartfelt friend, while a kind joke suits a friend who loves to laugh.

How long should an engagement wish be?

A few warm sentences are plenty — what matters is that your happiness for them shines through.

When should I send an engagement congratulations message?

As soon as you hear the news, while the excitement is fresh and your message will mean the most.

What should I say if I have not met my friend's fiancé yet?

Congratulate them both warmly and say you cannot wait to meet them — your excitement for your friend is what counts.
