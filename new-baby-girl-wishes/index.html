New Baby Girl Wishes

Here are 28 original wishes to welcome a new baby girl, grouped sweet, funny and short so you can find the right words fast. Tap Copy on any wish to send it in a card, a message or a gift note.

A new baby girl turns an ordinary week into a story the family will tell for years, and the right few words can make the parents smile every time they read them. Pick a wish that fits how close you are to the family, add the baby's name if you know it, and send it while the happy news is still fresh.

These wishes are written for a happy, healthy arrival. If the birth was difficult or the family is going through a hard time, keep your message gentle, skip the jokes and simply let them know you are thinking of them.

How to make it personal

A wish lands better when it sounds like it came from you, not from a card rack. A few small touches turn any message on this page into one the parents will keep.

  • Use the baby's name, and the parents' names too, so the message reads like it was written for this family alone.
  • Mention one real detail, like the colour of the blanket you sent, the first photo you saw or the hospital visit you are planning.
  • Match the tone to your relationship: sweet and tender for close family, light and funny for friends, warm and simple for colleagues.
  • If you are sending a gift, tie the wish to it, such as wishing her many cosy naps in the blanket you chose.
  • Keep it short if you are writing in a card, and save the longer stories for a message or a visit.

Questions people ask

What do you write in a card for a new baby girl?

Keep it warm and simple: congratulate the parents, welcome the baby by name if you know it, and wish the family health and happy days. A short, honest sentence beats a long, fancy one every time.

Should I mention the baby's name in my wish?

Yes, if you know it. Using her name makes the message feel personal and shows you were paying attention. If the name has not been shared yet, a simple little one or sweet girl works just as well.

Is it okay to send a funny wish for a new baby?

Yes, as long as it is gentle and kind. Jokes about lost sleep and tiny socks usually land well with close friends and family. Save the humour for people who know your sense of humour.

How long should a new baby wish be?

One or two sentences is plenty for a card, and a short paragraph suits a message. The parents are busy and tired, so a wish they can read in a few seconds is a kindness in itself.

When is the best time to send a new baby wish?

Within the first week is ideal, while the excitement is fresh. If you are late, send it anyway with a quick sorry for the delay, because a late wish still means a lot.
