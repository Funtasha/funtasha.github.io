These wishes are starting points, not finished cards. A line lands best when it carries a detail only you know — a favourite date, an inside joke, or the moment you knew he was special.

How to make it your own

A wish becomes yours the moment you add something only you could write. Try one of these before you send it:

  • Name a memory: a favourite date, a trip together, or the moment you knew he was special.
  • Mention an inside joke or a habit you love — the small things say more than grand words.
  • Sign it the way you always do — a nickname or a phrase only the two of you use.

Questions people ask

What should I write in a Valentine's card for my boyfriend?

Start with a wish from the romantic section, then add one detail only you know — a favourite date, an inside joke, or a hope for the year ahead. Two or three heartfelt sentences are plenty.

How long should a Valentine's message be?

Two or three sentences is enough for a card, and one sentence is enough for a text. A short message that is personal beats a long one that could be for anyone.

Can I send these wishes by text message?

Yes. The short wishes are written to fit a text, and the copy button copies the whole wish so you can paste it straight into your message.

What if we have only been together a short time?

Choose a short wish and add one small detail, like a favourite date or a joke you share. A light, warm message is perfect for a new relationship.

How do I make a Valentine's wish more personal?

Add a shared memory or an inside joke, and sign it the way you always do. The wish gives you the words; the personal detail makes them yours.
