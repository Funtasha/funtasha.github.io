How to make it your own

The best birthday wish sounds like you, not like a greetings card. Use one of these as a starting point, then make it yours:

  • Mention something he is proud of - a match, a project or a new skill.
  • Add a memory from a recent visit, like a game you played or something you built together.
  • Tell him what you admire about who he is becoming, not just what he does.
  • If he is a teenager, keep it warm but not babyish - he will notice the respect.

Questions people ask

What should I write in a birthday card for my grandson?

Tell him something you admire about him - his bravery, his curiosity or his kindness - and share a happy memory from a recent visit. Grandchildren love hearing that you notice who they are.

How can I make a birthday wish for my grandson more personal?

Mention his hobbies, his favourite sport or a project he is proud of. A detail from his own world shows you listen, and that makes the message special.

What is a good birthday wish for a teenage grandson?

Keep it warm and genuine without sounding babyish. Praise something real, like his hard work or his sense of humour, and let him know you are proud of the young man he is becoming.

Should a grandparent's birthday wish be funny or serious?

A little of both is ideal. A sincere message with one gentle joke feels natural and shows your love alongside your sense of fun.

What if I cannot be there for my grandson's birthday?

Call or video call on the day, and post a card early so it arrives in time. Tell him you are thinking of him and mention a plan you look forward to sharing soon.
